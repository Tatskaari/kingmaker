## Tamsin's backstory and the lore check

As written: "I'm the King's nephew, though nobody at court will say so. I owe Fat Jory three crowns."

Check 1: 1 issue, each fix taken.

- "I'm the King's nephew, though nobody at court will say so.": Being the King's nephew is a significant tie to the King that the lore would have recorded; the premise also says the King only asked for you this morning and the story's named people don't know you yet.
  - The fix: "I kept the palace books, though nobody at court will say they needed me."

Check 2, with the fixes in: it fits.

With the fixes in: "I kept the palace books, though nobody at court will say they needed me. I owe Fat Jory three crowns."

What it says of her: Tamsin Vell is a disgraced palace auditor who kept the palace books. She owes Fat Jory three crowns; why she took this case is not stated.
