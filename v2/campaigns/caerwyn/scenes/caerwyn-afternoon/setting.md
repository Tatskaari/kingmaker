Caerwyn's market square and the streets off it, afternoon on the eve of the Centennial Assembly: bunting going up on the procession route, sweepers, delegation banners (Nine Furrows wheat-sheaves, Saltmere salt-gulls, Kläggenheim axes), bread dearer than last week and grumbled about, the dead-cart's bell somewhere behind, the Corn Exchange posting prices, and everybody too busy for a hanged clerk.
