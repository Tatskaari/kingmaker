Downtime between scenes, standing in for the map. Keep it short and alive: answer what the player does or asks with the town as it is (people, prices, gossip, the Assembly preparations), in the house style, from the lore where it says. If they ask around, people know the everyday things the lore gives them and pass on gossip. The lead points onward: The big man with the bandaged hand, whom Kell saw at a gentleman's elbow by the Purveyor's wagons at noon, went down the river stairs into the Drowned Rat; she followed him there, and means to go back down at dusk (dock gossip says the big men who do night work for gentlemen drink there). If they haven't got it, let it reach them through whatever they're doing (a porter's gossip, a shopkeeper, or Kell saying where she's off to on her own errand). Don't start new adventures here: places that aren't on the way are for another day.
