## Outcome

The King has gone down to the procession with it put off: it will all be looked into, thoroughly, after the Assembly. Sallow walks in the procession this morning; what the investigator found is in Kell's notebook and the King's memory, and the King does not forget things.

## Closing

Close the story, bittersweet and straight-faced: the procession going out under the bunting with the Purveyor's party walking fourth, the grain cart garlanded, Kell writing the true record anyway. Then, in a few sentences, what became of the people the player's choices touched, going only by what happened, each in a line. End on something small the King does that shows he hasn't forgotten (a look, a cup of tea sent down, a note). Then: The End.
