Master Sallow is here, a minute behind his lavender, with his lantern-boy and the Crakes: in at the land door to see the last sacks down, take his share and initial tonight's page in the true book. Everything on the floor changes: the gang work faster and quieter, Scrope stands, Saul holds the door, Duchess growls, and Hobb (if he's here) can't look at anyone. If he has been warned, he's sharp and hurried, wants the book aboard the barge, and has the Crakes look along the wharf. If he finds the player held, or finds them at all, he deals with them his way: his office first, then money, then the Crakes and the river. This is the climax: hide and watch him sign, catch him in the act before witnesses who'll swear (Kell, Hep's gang, Scrope, Hobb), take the book from under his nose, bluff him, or be caught by him. The barge goes at the second bell, whoever has said what.

## Cast

- sallow: You want the barge loaded, the page initialled and your share in your purse. Find fault with something, then climb to the counting-box and initial tonight's page at Scrope's desk, in front of whoever is on the floor. If you find intruders: papers, then money, then the Crakes. Keep the book out of their hands. Exposed in front of witnesses you can't buy, with a sergeant saying the words of arrest and your Crakes still at your side, you don't come quietly: the sword comes out of the cane, and you set the Crakes on them.
- kell: This is the investigator's case and their moment: back their play. You can arrest Sallow if you see him take the King's grain, by the book and out loud, and you will when the investigator calls for it or plainly gives you the moment; never over their plan or instead of it. If they mean to slip away with the proof, go with them. If you're on the wharf, come at their signal or the first shout.
- hep: Keep the gang working and your head down, and watch who looks likeliest to keep your gang off the gallows.
- scrope: Present the book to the Master with shaking hands.
- hobb: If you're here, you can't look at the Master, or at the player.
- saul: Hold the land door for the Master, and keep Duchess off his ankles.

## Pressure

If he hasn't yet, Sallow climbs to the counting-box: Scrope presents the book with shaking hands, and Sallow initials tonight's page and counts his heron pennies into his purse, in plain view of the floor (the moment to catch him at it, for anyone who means to). Then he takes out his watch: the ebb won't wait. He orders the last sacks down and the hatches battened, and out on the river the bargees start singling up their lines.
