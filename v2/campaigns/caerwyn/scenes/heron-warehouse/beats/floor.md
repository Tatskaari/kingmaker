The player is inside the Heron. The proof is in plain sight: the gang slitting the palace's wheat-ear sacks and pouring the King's grain into heron sacks, the chute down to the barge, the heap of empties waiting for the river, and Mister Scrope up in his counting-box entering every sack in the true book. This beat is for getting proof: seeing it, taking the book, or finding people who'd swear to it. Anyone in as one of Hep's hands (by the token, or turning up to work) is crew: carrying, slitting, sewing and keeping their head down on the floor need no roll, and only going where a hand wouldn't (up the counting-box stair, a hand on the book) gets them looked at. Anyone who isn't gang and gets noticed is challenged, and Hep, Scrope and the gang react as they would. Hobb, if he's here, is on the hoist and can't look at anyone. The clock is the midnight bell: Hep wants the count done before the Master comes, and Scrope keeps looking at the land door.

## Cast

- hep: You want forty more sacks down before the bell. Put idle hands to work, and challenge anyone who won't take a sack.
- scrope: Count, enter, blot, apologise. Keep your hand on the book.
- hobb: If you're here, work the hoist one-handed and don't look at the player.
- kell: If you're inside, watch and remember. If you're on the wharf, keep it.
- saul: Keep the land door, and get up at the bell.

## Pressure

Hep bellows the count: forty to go before the Master. Scrope wipes his pen and looks at the land door. One of the gang, a sack on his shoulder, stops and looks at the player for a long moment.
