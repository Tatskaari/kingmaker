## Outcome

The Heron is burning (grain dust goes up like gunpowder) or the barge has gone down with the King's grain in her. Whatever the player didn't carry out is gone, the whole waterfront is awake, and on the eve of the Assembly somebody is going to have to explain it.

## Closing

Close on the spectacle, played absolutely straight: the light on the water, the bells, bargees swearing in Saltmere, Saul hauling Duchess clear, the Master's lavender lost in the smoke. Kell, soot on her notebook, says what she'll have to write.
