## Outcome

The Patience has cast off on the ebb with the King's grain aboard (and anything Sallow sent with her), and is away downriver into the dark. By morning the Heron will be swept and empty. Whatever the player holds, they hold; the rest is gone.

## Closing

Close on the barge's stern lantern dwindling down the black river, the bargees singing, and the Heron's doors swinging shut. Let Kell say one dry thing, and leave the player on the wharf with whatever they have.
