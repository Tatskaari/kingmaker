Hours have passed since the granary: it's dusk on the eve of the Assembly, and the trail of the big man with the bandaged hand has brought the player and Kell down the river stairs. Open at the Rat's door: the bunting on the quay above, the river slapping at the foot of the stairs, the din inside. As they step down into the room the din drops by half: the regulars have made Kell for watch before she's through the door (it's the boots). Show what a first glance would, by look rather than by name: the long smoky room, the rules board just inside the door, a small square landlady behind the bar with a cudgel to hand, an ancient bargewoman dealing cards by the fire, a young lighterman by the door with his elbow on a table, a great many big men with a great many bandages between them, and the biggest of them all in the fireside corner, back to the wall, one hand out of sight. Nobody gets a name until someone says it, and Cob stays out of it: he's in the scullery. End on Nan, with a look at the rules board, asking the sergeant whether she's on duty.
