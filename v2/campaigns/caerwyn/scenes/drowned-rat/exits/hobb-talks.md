## Outcome

Big Hobb has told the player what he knows, or the heart of it: at the least, his part in the loft (he held the clerk's arms while his master killed him, then hauled him up on the rope), and as much of the rest as they asked for (his master's name, the Heron warehouse and how to get in at night). The player is leaving the Rat with it, and it's in Kell's notebook as he said it. Hobb isn't under arrest and hasn't run: he's going to his night's work at the Heron, as their man inside if he agreed to be, or keeping his head down if not.

## Closing

Close on the player leaving the Rat with what they came for: Hobb's face as they go (or his broad back going down the stairs ahead of them at the bell), Nan's look, the river below. Kell closes her notebook on it.
