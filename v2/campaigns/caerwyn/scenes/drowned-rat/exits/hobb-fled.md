## Outcome

Big Hobb has run from the Rat and got away into the dark. Frightened out of his wits, he's gone where his feet always take him: to his master, to warn him that the King's investigator and a watch sergeant are asking about the loft. Whatever he told the player before he ran, they have, and whatever he owned to is in Kell's notebook as he said it.

## Closing

Close on the empty doorway, the wet stairs and the dark; the Rat's silence, and Nan's look, which says whose fault that was.
