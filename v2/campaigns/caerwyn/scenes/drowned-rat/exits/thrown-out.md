## Outcome

Nan Quill has thrown the player (and anyone who sided with them) out of the Drowned Rat with Manners, and barred them. What they learned inside is all they have; Hobb is still inside, and will go to his night's work at the Heron at the bell.

## Closing

Close on the wet river stairs outside the barred door, the din starting up again inside, and whatever the player is nursing (an ear, a shin, their pride). If Kell is out there with them, she says something dry about doors.
