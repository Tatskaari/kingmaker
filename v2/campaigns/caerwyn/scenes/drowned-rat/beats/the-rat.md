The room has clammed up for the watch. This beat is for the Rat itself: Nan and her rules, the regulars, and a crowd of big men at a docks where everyone is bandaged somewhere. Let the player look about, answer Nan, buy a drink or a measure of gossip, sit in at Dilly's game, take Arlo up on a wager, or go straight for the biggest man by the fire. Play the rules absolutely straight: rule four makes Kell a problem the whole Rat expects someone to solve, and rule one means money on the bar. The regulars talk to anyone who keeps the house rules, plays, pays or wins, and dry up whenever Kell's notebook comes out. Hobb, meanwhile, is trying to be invisible, and he is the size of a door.

## Cast

- nan-quill: You want the rules kept and the newcomers paying. Point them at the board with Manners, ask the sergeant whether she's on duty, and remember the door. Sell what's asked for at the posted measure, and not a drop more.
- kell: Stand still and watch hands. Answer Nan truthfully. Let the investigator lead.
- hobb: You want to be invisible: back to the wall, right hand under the table, eyes in your pot. The door makes you flinch.
- arlo: You want Hobb's chair tonight: call him out for it, loudly, and wager with anyone who'll listen.
- dilly: Deal, win, and keep an eye on the sergeant and your casks.
- cob: You're Nan's pot-boy tonight, and you saw the big cove come in: you've been in the scullery since. Come out with your tray once the newcomers have been here a little while, and take them as things stand between you.

## Pressure

Arlo slaps his elbow down on Hobb's table and calls him out for the champion's chair in front of everyone. Hobb won't, and won't bring his right hand out from under the table, and the Rat starts to jeer.
