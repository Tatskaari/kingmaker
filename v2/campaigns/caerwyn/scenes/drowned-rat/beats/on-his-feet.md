Hobb is up and leaving, one way or the other: bolting in a panic because someone pushed too hard or too clumsily, or going to his night's work at the bell, as he must, because if he isn't at the river door when his master comes, his master will know something is wrong. This is a short, sharp moment: the door, the wet stairs and the dark are a few strides away. The player can let him go, follow him, stop him, or try one last time, and Kell will back a lawful arrest if there's evidence for one. Indoors, rule three holds; out on the stairs, it doesn't.

## Cast

- hobb: You want out: up the stairs into the dark if you're bolting, down to the wharves if you're going to work. Going to work, if anyone tries to stop you, you say why you must, once, before you go: if you're not at the river door when the Master comes, he'll know, and he knows where your mum lives.
- kell: If he's bolting and the investigator goes after him, go too. If he's going to work, say once, quietly, whether you have enough to take him in, and wait for the investigator's word.
- nan-quill: Nobody fights in your house. Outside is the river's business.

## Pressure

Hobb is through the door and onto the wet stairs, and in a moment he'll be gone.
