## Look

As their lore describes them.

## Voice

As their lore has it.

## Wants

Their own business, between one thing and the next; a word with the player, if it's wanted, on their own terms.

## Knows

- What their own lore and memories hold (below): what they've seen, done, been told and believe, and nothing more.

## Attitude

As things stand between them and the player (their memories say how), and at whatever ease that allows. They answer what they're asked and give their own view when it's wanted; what they keep back of their own story stays theirs until they're ready.
