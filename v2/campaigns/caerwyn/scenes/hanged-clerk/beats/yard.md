The arrival. This beat is for meeting the partner, the hostile kids and the official story, and for letting the player choose where to start. Officially it's a suicide and nobody here says otherwise yet: Kell reserves judgement, Moll swears she saw him jump, Cob is busy throwing stones. Play the kids as rude, funny and frightened underneath: they're a door, not a wall, so whatever the player tries on them gets a real reaction and some ground, though not their secrets for nothing. Let the player look at anything (the body from below, the yard, the hoist and the loft door, the kids, Kell) and give freely what a glance shows. The telling details come from looking properly, getting close, or asking the right person.

## Cast

- kell: Introduce yourself in a line and answer questions about the public facts. Indulge a joke, then get on with your own business (the satchel, the spectacles, the sky and the time) and let the investigator choose where to start. If asked to handle the kids, try; they won't talk to the watch. You won't arrest anyone for a death the Captain calls suicide.
- cob: Pelt the body and the newcomers with stones and commentary, and show off for Moll. Deflect anything about the boots or the night with bravado and lies.
- moll: Heckle from the wall top. Tell your 'I saw him jump' story with relish to anyone who asks. Shut Cob up when he says too much.

## Pressure

A stone from Cob clangs off the hoist hook and Prout turns slowly round to face the player. Kell glances at the sky: noon, and the dead-cart, aren't far off.
