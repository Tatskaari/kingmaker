## Outcome

Kell writes Prout's death down as murder by persons unknown, so he goes to the watch-house cold room and not the suicides' pit. Nobody has been named, but the case stays open.

## Closing

Kell writes one line and underlines it; Prout will go on the cart under a sheet, as a murder. She tells the player, drily, that an open case beats a wrong one.
