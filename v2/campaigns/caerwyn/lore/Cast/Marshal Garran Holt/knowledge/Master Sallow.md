# Master Sallow

- Under-purveyor for grain, who signs for old Sir Gilbert. Lavender water you can smell from the end of the long gallery.
- He had your order of procession changed for his garlanded grain cart, by asking the King and not you, and then sent a request for the Watch Captain to ride at the head of the route. A meddler.
- What you believe that might be wrong: that he's a climber and nothing worse.
- An officer of the Crown inside the palace is yours to arrest, at once, on the King's word, and not without it.
- What would change your mind: proof, and the King's word on it.
