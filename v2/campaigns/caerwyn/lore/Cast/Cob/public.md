# Cob

A granary rat: one of the children who run errands, pinch things and sleep rough round the yards of Caerwyn's granary quarter. Does pots at the Drowned Rat on busy nights, for Nan Quill.

## Look
About eleven and scrawny, in a man's waistcoat. Scabbed knuckles, a bruise or two, and a stone in each fist. Talks about himself as "Cob", at the top of his voice.

## Known for
- Throwing things, accurately.
- Swearing inventively, and never quite obscenely.
- Going about with Moll, who bosses him.
- His father, Nat Pike, is a porter at the granary with a thirst. When Cob has a new bruise, people round the granary don't ask.
