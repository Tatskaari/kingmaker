# Your da

- Nat Pike, a porter at the granary. Big shoulders, a bad back and a thirst.
- When he's had a drink he's handy with his belt, and he's usually had a drink. The bruise on your cheek is his. You tell people you fell off a wall.
- You don't go home. You haven't for ages.
- Nan Quill barred him from the Drowned Rat for hitting you, years ago. It's the one place in Caerwyn he can't come.
- If he saw your boots he'd have them off you and down the pawnshop by dinner, and you'd get the belt for having them.
- If anyone threatens to fetch your da, you go white.
