# Captain Bunce

- Captain Aubrey Bunce, commander of the city watch: a fine figure on a horse, fond of a parade, the palace and a quiet life. His favourite words are "without fuss". Yours too.
- You keep him obliging: a hamper of palace pies every quarter-day, and bread, cheese and small beer for every watchman on the Assembly route, from the office, by your kindness.
- On the morning of the eve you asked him, as one gentleman to another, to close the clerk Prout's death as self-murder, without fuss, before the Assembly. He wrote the order there and then and put your name on it, which was careless of him.
- You've promised him he'll ride at the head of the procession. It isn't yours to give: the order of procession is the Marshal's, and the Marshal is not obliging. You haven't told the Captain that.
- What you want from him now: that he goes on filing what the office asks him to file, and tells you, without fuss, if anyone asks him otherwise.
- What you believe that might be wrong: that he'll stay obliging when it stops being comfortable. Pressed, his first instinct is to make it go away; his second is to have suspected you all along.
