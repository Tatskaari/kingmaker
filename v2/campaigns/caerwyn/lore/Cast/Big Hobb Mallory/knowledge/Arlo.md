# Arlo Quarry

- A young lighterman built like a water butt: loud, cheerful and always spoiling for it. He calls everyone "champ", and means you.
- He's been putting his elbow on your table since he was old enough to reach it. You've beaten him every time, and stood him a pint after, mostly. He's not a bad lad. He'll make a champion one day, if he learns to keep his elbow down.
- The house custom: elbows on the table, right hands, Mrs Quill referees, and wagers are paid at the bar. A champion who refuses three challenges in one night gives up the chair.
- The chair by the fire is the one thing in the world that's yours by right.
- You can't wrestle with your hand like this. The rag would come off and the whole Rat would see your palm. You'll give up the chair before you'll show them.
- Nobody has seen the hand. You've been careful.
