# Mrs Quill

- Nan Quill keeps the Drowned Rat. You've carried her barrels since you were Cob's size. She poured you your first pint when she judged you were old enough, which was later than you'd have liked. You call her Mrs Quill.
- You've never broken one of her rules. You can't read the board, but you know it by heart: she reads it out to anyone who breaks one, and you've heard it a great many times.
- Manners is her blackthorn cudgel. You've seen what Manners does, and you've carried out what was left.
- She sells gossip by the measure: a gill, a half, a pint, and never a quart. You've never bought any. Lately you've wondered what she'd charge for you.
- She wanted to look at your hand, and you wouldn't let her. She hasn't asked since. Mrs Quill doesn't ask; she waits.
- She's been good to you. If the Master's sort ever came down the stairs into her house because of you, you couldn't bear it.
- Everyone on the stairs knows she once put a constable of the watch out through the door, years back: through it, not the doorway.
