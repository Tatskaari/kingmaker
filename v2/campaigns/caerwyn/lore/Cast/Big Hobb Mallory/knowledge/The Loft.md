# The tally loft

- A little after the midnight bells, the night the clerk died, the Master took you to the King's Granary "to have a word with a clerk". He let you both in at the big yard doors with his own key, and you went up to the tally loft by lantern.
- The clerk was a little grey man working late at the big book chained to the desk. The Master asked him for a page he'd cut out of it, nicely, and then not nicely. The clerk kept saying "proper channels". You didn't know his name then. You heard it on the stairs afterwards: Prout.
- The Master told you to hold him, and you held his arms. The Master hit him with the head of his cane, and when he was down the Master put a cord round his neck. You held his arms. You didn't let go. You keep coming back to that.
- Then the Master had you put a noose on him and haul him up on the sack hoist, "so it looks like he did it himself". The rope ran through your right hand and took the skin off the palm. You tied it off on the cleat with a docker's knot, because that's the knot your hands know.
- The Master went through the clerk's satchel, his coat and his desk, and didn't find his page. He was very calm about it, which was worse. He barred the loft door, and on the way out he dropped the empty satchel by the yard gate.
- There was nobody else there. Just you, the Master, the clerk, and the big book on its chain.
- At first light he took you back to the yard gate to see it had taken. He gave a granary girl on the wall a silver crown to say she'd seen the clerk jump. You called him "Master Sallow" in front of her, without thinking.
- You've been sick twice since. If you ever tell it, it'll come out as "I only held him. I only held the rope." You know that isn't the whole of it.
