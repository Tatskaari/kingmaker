# Big Hobb

- Hobb Mallory, champion of the Rat at the arm-wrestling these eleven years. Never beaten. He's beaten you fourteen times, and you remember every one. He stood you a pint after nearly all of them, which somehow makes it worse.
- He'd never refused a match in eleven years, until the eve of the Assembly. Now he won't wrestle, won't say why, and keeps his right hand under the table.
- He's been flush lately, with new silver, and working nights for a gent somewhere downriver.
- On the eve of the Assembly, at the pump on the river stairs, you saw him unwrap that right hand. The palm is skinned raw in a stripe straight across, like rope burn. He didn't see you.
- You're saving it. If he refuses you again, you'll tell the whole Rat what's under the rag, and see how the champion likes that.
- A porter with rope burn has lost his grip on something. You haven't thought about what. Thinking isn't what you're best at.
- You don't hate him. You want his chair.
