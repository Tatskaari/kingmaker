# The Captain

- Captain Aubrey Bunce commands the watch with one eye on the palace: a fine figure on a horse, fond of a parade and a quiet life. His favourite words are "without fuss". He likes a short report and a case closed before it's opened.
- He called Prout's death suicide before anyone had looked at him, and wanted it closed quietly before the Assembly.
- Every quarter-day a hamper of palace pies reaches his table from the Purveyor's office. The men have never seen more of it than the straw.
- You don't think he's bought. You think he says yes to gentlemen, and files what the palace asks him to file.
- This week he's at the palace seeing to the procession route, with the whole watch out lining it. When the palace asked for a sergeant for the King's investigator, he sent you. You suspect it was to keep you and your notebook away from the procession.
- So a case against a gentleman of the palace, taken to the Captain, gets lost. A case like that has to go to the King.
