# Cob

- A granary boy of eleven or so who does your pots on busy nights, for a penny and a bowl of eel stew. Quick with a tray, useless with a mop, and loud. He talks about himself as "Cob", and swears more inventively than any bargee in the house.
- His da is Nat Pike, a granary porter with a thirst. Years ago he hit the boy in the Rat, and you barred him for it, for life. The Rat is the one place in Caerwyn his da can't come, which is why the boy feels safe here.
- He sleeps rough somewhere round the granary yards. You've never asked where, and he's never said.
- The scullery's slop hatch opens onto the river, and it's just big enough for him. You've told him what the river does to boys.
- You'd not see him hurt in your house. You don't say so.
