# Sergeant Kell

- On the morning of the eve you asked Captain Bunce, through a page, for a sergeant of the watch to go with your investigator. He sent one Agnes Kell. You've never met her.
- You assume the Captain sent his best. The Captain is very obliging.
- What you'd want from her now: what she saw, sworn, so that it isn't only the investigator's word, or yours.
- What you believe that might be wrong: that nobody in the watch had heard of Wendell's trouble before he died, and that you alone put him off.
- What would change your mind: her telling you otherwise. If she owns up to something of her own about Wendell, you'll find you can own up too.
