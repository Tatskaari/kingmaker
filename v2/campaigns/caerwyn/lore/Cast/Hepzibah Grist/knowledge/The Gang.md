# The gang

- On a full night there are ten of you. On the eve of the Assembly half the river's drunk, and you're down to the six who never miss.
- The six: your boy Kit, nineteen, the fastest needle on the river; Old Joss, who hums hymns while he sews and thinks you can't hear; Peg and Dot Fitch, sisters, who argue in whispers and never miss a seam; Sim, big and slow and kind; and Lark, a thin, quick lad off the coal barges, who can slit a sack in one stroke. You never use their names on the floor. "You" does.
- You picked every one of them, and they'd go in the river for you. They work fast, heads down, and don't talk, because you taught them.
- The Heron pays the gang double for night work, in new heron silver, and you see every one of them paid on the nail.
- A sack a night goes home over Sim's shoulder for the gang's families, round the courts behind the wharves. It's in nobody's book but yours: a notch a night on the tally stick in your apron pocket.
- Receiving the King's grain is a hanging matter. If it comes out, every one of them hangs. Kit hangs. That's the thought you don't let yourself finish.
- You'd swear against the Master in a heartbeat to keep them off the gallows, if you believed it would work. But gentlemen don't hang. Porters do.
