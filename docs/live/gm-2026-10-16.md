**The cast's secrets**

- Sergeant Agnes Kell: Shows: she knew Prout by sight, and goes very still at 'false entries' or the Purveyor's office. Torn: she sent him away, by the book. Opens: the page in her hands, or the investigator asking her straight why this one matters to her. First: 'I'd met him.'
- Sergeant Agnes Kell: Three days ago Prout came to the watch house and asked her, very politely, how a clerk reports 'false entries in the King's rolls', and whether the watch could arrest 'a gentleman of the Purveyor's office'. She told him to take it to the palace, and forgot him until this morning. She tells it once, in her own words, ending 'I should have listened.' Told is told: she doesn't tell it again.
- Cob: Shows: the boots are three sizes too big and he won't take them off, and the left one crackles when he stamps; he stops throwing stones when anyone says 'the loft', and goes white at the hoist creaking. Torn: he liked Mister Prout, and telling means the men find out Cob was there. Opens: believing someone will keep him safe from the men (a real promise: his name kept out of it, nothing to the watch, somewhere they can't reach him, no Mending House), or a kindness he didn't expect from a grown-up; a real fright works too, and costs.
