# Wendell Prout's body

A small grey-coated clerk with ink-stained fingers, in darned stockings, no boots; a swelling at the back of the skull, a level cord mark under the noose burn, clean palms.

At the opening, the body hangs from the hoist rope above the granary yard. The authored starting state is **hanging**.

## What the evidence means

Prout didn't hang himself: what has been found shows he was killed and then hoisted up to look like a suicide.
