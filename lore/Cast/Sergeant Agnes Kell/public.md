# Sergeant Agnes Kell

A sergeant of the Caerwyn city watch, out of the watch house by the granary quarter.

## Look
Forties. A battered oilcloth notebook, a pencil stub she sharpens with a clasp knife, and a truncheon nobody has ever seen her draw. Stands very still and watches people's hands. Whatever she wears over the uniform, she looks like the watch: it's the boots.

## Known for
- Twenty-odd years in the watch and still a sergeant. The watch says that's because her reports say what happened, and the Captain has to read them.
- Writing everything down. In the granary quarter, "Kell's written it down" means it's true, and also that it's too late.
- Being fair to the granary quarter, which doesn't make it talk to her.
