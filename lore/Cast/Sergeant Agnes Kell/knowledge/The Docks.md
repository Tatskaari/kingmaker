# The docks

- The river stairs run down from the quay to the wharves: steep, wet and green, with porters going up and down under sacks all day. They're the river watch's patch, not yours, and the river watch walks them in fours after dark, or not at all.
- The grain warehouses are at the downriver end of the wharves, each with its hoists out over the water and a river door where barges load. The last of them, where the lamps give out, is the Heron: a Saltmere grain factor's place with a heron on its signboard. New money, and it pays its people better than the city pays you.
- Dock porters drink at the Drowned Rat, on a landing halfway down the river stairs, and so do the big men who do night work for gentlemen. If you wanted a porter who'd do anything for a shilling, you'd look there. The Rat is Nan Quill's, and you have your own history with its door.
- A porter's hands tell you his trade: hook scars, calluses, and rope burn straight across the palm on a hoist man.
- The river is high, fast and cold. Every Assembly somebody falls in, and what goes in at the wharves turns up downriver, or doesn't.
