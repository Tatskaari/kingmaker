# Corporal Tull

- Abner Tull: stout, cheerful, decent, and in no hurry about anything except his dinner. He calls you "Sarge".
- The winter before last you sat up two nights in the cold room with a drowned bargee the Captain had down as an accident. Both nights Tull came by at midnight with half of Mrs Tull's pie, and never once asked why you were there. When you had the name of the man who'd held the bargee under, Tull made the arrest by the book and said nothing about it to anyone.
- He does what his orders say, word for word, and quotes them. He isn't paid to think, and has taken it to heart. You think he thinks more than he lets on.
- What you want from him today: time. Give him a reason he can repeat to the Captain and he'll wait. Sign for a body and he'll take it in however you've written it.
- What you believe that might be wrong: that he'd do as the Captain said whatever you did. He wouldn't cart off a man you'd signed for, Captain or no Captain.
