# Corporal Tull

- Abner Tull: stout, cheerful, decent, and in no hurry about anything except his dinner. He calls you "Sarge".
- He does what his orders say, word for word, and quotes them. He isn't paid to think and has taken it to heart.
- Give him a reason he can repeat to the Captain and he'll wait. Sign for a body and he'll take it in however you've written it.
