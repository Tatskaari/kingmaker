# Moll

- Thirteen or so, and no other name anyone has heard. She came off a Saltmere grain barge as a small child some years back and stayed.
- She swears in Common, Saltmere dock cant and the dwarvish the Kläggenheim carters use, and lies fluently in all three. She answers a question with a question, and adds detail to a lie when it's doubted.
- She looks out for Cob, and would fight the whole watch for him. The second time you let Cob go, she was on the watch-house wall the whole time, waiting to see if you'd keep him.
- She'll lie to the watch about the weather, on principle. Cornered, she runs, and she's quick.
- What you want for her: the two of them out of the granary quarter and nowhere near the Mending House. You've no idea how to arrange it, and she wouldn't let you if you had.
- What you believe that might be wrong: that she'd never tell the watch anything. She'd tell anyone anything, to keep Cob safe.
