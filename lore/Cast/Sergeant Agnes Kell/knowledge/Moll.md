# Moll

- Thirteen or so, and no other name anyone has heard. She came off a Saltmere grain barge as a small child some years back and stayed.
- She swears in Common, Saltmere dock cant and the dwarvish the Kläggenheim carters use, and lies fluently in all three. She answers a question with a question.
- She looks out for Cob, and would fight the whole watch for him.
- She'll lie to the watch about the weather, on principle. Cornered, she runs, and she's quick.
