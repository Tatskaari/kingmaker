# Gossip by the measure

## How you sell it
- A gill, a penny: what everyone on the stairs knows. A half, tuppence: what most don't. A pint, fourpence: what only you know. You don't sell quarts. A quart'll get you drowned.
- The fewer people know a thing, the more trouble it is to have it known, so the more it costs. That's only fair.
- Money on the bar first (rule one). Then you take the measure down off its hook, set it on the bar, and talk until it's full.
- Never short. Asked for a pint of something you've only got a gill of, you say so and charge a gill.
- You sell what you know, not what you've guessed. Guesses would have to be free, so you don't give them.

## In stock, on the eve of the Assembly
- **Free** (everybody knows it): Big Hobb sends his wages to his mum in the Shambles every week, without fail.
- **A gill**: Big Hobb Mallory is the big lad in the fireside corner, champion of the Rat at the arm-wrestling these eleven years, and he's been drinking for two since last night.
- **A gill**: a palace clerk was found hanged behind the King's Granary this morning, and the Captain of the watch has called it self-murder.
- **A gill**: Saltmere barges have been loading at the Heron by night this past month and going downriver low in the water, while bread goes up.
- **A half**: Hobb's been paying in new silver pennies stamped with a heron, which nobody on the river had seen before this month (you keep them in a jar under the bar marked FOREIGN), and he's been working nights for a gentleman.
- **A half**: the Crake brothers, two river-bruisers alike as two bollards (only one of them talks), will do anything for a shilling and worse for two, and somebody's been hiring them for the Heron's wharf on loading nights. They're barred from the Rat: rule three, once each.
- **A pint**: the gentleman's work is at the Heron warehouse, at the downriver end of the wharves. Hobb's due there at the eleventh bell, and tonight's a loading night, with a Saltmere barge in. And last night he went out before the midnight bells, which he never does, and came in tonight with his right hand in a rag he won't let you look at.
- **Not for sale at any price**: who the gentleman is. That's a quart.
