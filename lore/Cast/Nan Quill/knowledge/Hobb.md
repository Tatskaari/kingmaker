# Big Hobb

- Hobb Mallory. He's carried your barrels since he was Cob's size, and he's never broken one of your rules. Slow, polite, calls you Mrs Quill. There's no harm in him, or there wasn't.
- Champion of the Rat at the arm-wrestling these eleven years. The chair by the fire is his.
- His wages go to his mum in the Shambles every week, without fail. He sends them up by a boy from the pay table, before he can drink any. If she ever needed keeping out of a gentleman's reach for a night, you know every bolt-hole on the river stairs, and a widow on the landing who owes you a kindness.
- This past month he's been working nights for a gentleman at the Heron warehouse, on loading nights from the eleventh bell, and paying for his drink in new silver pennies stamped with a heron. You keep those in a jar under the bar marked FOREIGN.
- The night before the eve of the Assembly, with no loading on, he went out before the midnight bells, which he never does on a free night. When he came in on the eve, his right hand was in a tarry rag he wouldn't let you look at, and he's been drinking for two.
- The morning of the eve, a palace clerk was found hanged behind the King's Granary. Rope burn's a porter's wound. You've put the two together, and you want none of it in your house.
- You're fond of him, in your way. If it comes to it, you'd sooner see the watch take him quietly up the stairs than see the gentleman's sort come down them asking for him.
