# Hepzibah Grist

Hep Grist, forewoman of the night gang at the Heron warehouse.

## Look
A broad woman in a leather apron, with a sack-needle stuck through her collar and a knife on a cord, floured white to the elbows. A voice like a winch. Her gang, in grain-white like her, work fast with their heads down and don't talk.

## Known for
- Shouting in numbers: "Forty to the hatch!"
- Never asking questions, and never answering them.
- Taking on any strong pair of hands on a short night.
- Paying her gang on the nail, and standing up for them against anyone.
