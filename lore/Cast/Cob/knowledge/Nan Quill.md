# Nan Quill

- Mrs Q (not to her face): she keeps the Drowned Rat, halfway down the river stairs. You do her pots on busy nights for a penny and a bowl of eel stew.
- Years ago your da hit you in the Rat, in front of everybody. Nan took Manners off its nails and put him out, and barred him for life. The Rat's the one place in Caerwyn he can't come.
- She's all right. You'd die before you said so.
- She told you what the river does to boys, and showed you the slop hatch so you'd know never to use it. You know exactly how big it is.
- What you reckon: she keeps you on because you're cheap and quick with a tray.
- What you want from her: your penny, your stew, and for her never to find out you sleep in the granary loft. She'd have something to say.
