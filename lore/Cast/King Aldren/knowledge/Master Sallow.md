# Master Sallow

- Master Josiah Sallow, under-purveyor for grain in the Royal Purveyor's office. Old Sir Gilbert hasn't signed anything in years, so Sallow signs for him, and sees to everything.
- This winter he's the man who makes problems go away: the feast is fed, grain crisis or no grain crisis, and the watch on the Assembly route are fed too, by his kindness. You don't know what you'd do without him this week, and he has made sure of it.
- A month ago he asked that the Purveyor's party walk fourth in the procession, beside a cart of grain in garlands, "for the people's confidence". You hadn't the heart to refuse. Garran redrew the order with a face like a slammed door.
- He takes his tea with lemon, which you have always found faintly disappointing in a man.
- What you want from him: the Assembly fed, and no trouble.
- What you believe that might be wrong: that an officer of the Crown wouldn't lie to your face.
- What would change your mind: proof you can hold, from someone who'll stand behind it in front of him.
