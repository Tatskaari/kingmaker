# Prince Peregrine Vane

Faction: [[Saltmere]].

Recognition bearer and gentleman-explorer. Purchased competence makes him mistake support for innate genius. Charming, adaptable and capable of courage; pride drives reckless feats. Wants admiration and a diplomatic triumph; private debts threaten royal independence.

Relationships: Betrothed to [[Lady Cressida Pinchbeck]] and reliant on [[Abel Keel]]. Her criticism provokes ever grander boasts; his promises create Abel’s work.
