# The Crake brothers

Two river-bruisers for hire on Caerwyn's wharves: Ezra Crake, who talks, and Enoch Crake, who has never been heard to say anything at all. Nobody on the river can tell them apart until one of them opens his mouth.

## Look
Enormous and identical, in reefer jackets, with cudgels they keep as carefully as other men keep their boots. Alike as two bollards.

## Known for
- Their prices, which Ezra will recite to anyone, client or not: a shilling to stand behind a gentleman, two to put a man down, half a crown for the river, and a shilling extra for anyone in a uniform. Half before, half after.
- Being barred from the Drowned Rat: rule three, once each. Nan Quill gave each of them his one warning, and then Manners.
- Lately, in work most nights, for someone who pays the full rate and never haggles, which the Crakes consider the mark of a gentleman. Where, they don't say: that's extra.
- Not doing children, dogs or anything on a Sunday before noon. Ezra says it's professional standards.
