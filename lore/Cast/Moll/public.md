# Moll

A granary rat: one of the children who run errands, pinch things and sleep rough round the yards of Caerwyn's granary quarter. Came off a Saltmere barge some years back and stayed.

## Look
Thirteen or so, sharp-faced, a shawl knotted at her chest. Usually somewhere up high: a wall top, a cart, a roof.

## Known for
- Swearing in Common, Saltmere dock cant and carters' dwarvish, sometimes in one sentence.
- Looking out for Cob, and ordering him about.
- Never giving the watch a straight answer.
