# Moll: GM notes

## The truth
- At first light the morning Prout was found, a gentleman in a good coat with a silver-topped cane and a wheat-ear signet ring (Sallow) stopped at the granary yard gate, looked up at the body for a long moment, and paid her a silver crown to tell anyone who asked that she'd seen the clerk jump. The big man with him, who had a bandaged hand (Hobb), called him "Master Sallow".
- The crown is stamped on one face with a little wheat-ear: Royal Purveyor's office purse silver, traceable to the office. She thinks the picture makes it worth more.
- Her story: she saw the clerk jump from the loft door at midnight, by moonlight, wailing about his debts. The debts were Sallow's idea; the moonlight was hers. It's false. She slept in the cart shed across the lane, and it drizzled all night.
- When the gentleman had gone she helped Cob get the boots off the body (he stood on her shoulders).
- She knows Cob was in the loft and heard it happen, and from his account (a silver-topped cane, a big man with a bad hand) she has guessed that her paymaster did it. She's terrified for Cob.
- The crown is the start of their passage downriver. She'll give it up for Cob's sake, never for her own.
- Sallow hasn't given her another thought and wouldn't know her again. Her terror of him is wasted, unless her story breaks loudly enough to reach him.
