# Cob

- He's yours. You found him two winters ago under a cart with a split lip, and you've looked after him since. He's eleven and an idiot, and he's all you've got. His real name's Cuthbert. You only use it when he's being an idiot on purpose.
- His da's a porter at the granary and handy with his belt. Cob sleeps anywhere but home, mostly in his "castle" up in the tally loft.
- He's a terrible liar. He brags when he's scared, and the more scared, the louder.
- The night the clerk died, Cob was in the loft and heard it: Silvertop with his cane, the big cove, a thump, the hoist. He told you at first light, white as milk. You told him to shut it and never tell another soul. If those men find out he was there, they'll come for him. You'll do anything to stop that.
- You want him out of the granary quarter, out of the Mending House and out of his da's reach.
