# The gentleman

- At first light the morning the clerk was found, he stopped at the granary yard gate: a good coat, a silver-topped cane, a signet ring with a little wheat-ear on it, and enough lavender water to make your eyes run. He looked up at the clerk for a long moment, the way you'd look at a job done.
- The big man with him had a bandaged hand, and called him "Master Sallow".
- He gave you a silver crown to tell anyone who asked that you'd seen the clerk jump. It's more money than you've ever held, and it's got a little wheat-ear on one face, which you reckon makes it worth more.
- Your story: you saw the clerk jump from the loft door at midnight, by moonlight, wailing about his debts. He told you to say debts. The moonlight was your own idea. (You were asleep in the cart shed across the lane, and it drizzled all night.)
- Cob's Silvertop, from the loft, has a silver-topped cane, and the big cove with him had a bad hand. You're not stupid. Nobody pays you to say a man jumped unless he didn't.
- He'll come back to check you're telling it right. He'd know you again. If your story breaks, he'll know who broke it.
