# The Captain's orders

- The order in your pocket, in Captain Bunce's own hand, which you'll show to anyone who doubts you: "The death of W. Prout, clerk, to be closed as self-murder, without fuss, at the request of Master J. Sallow of the Royal Purveyor's office."
- You knew Prout to nod to: a little clerk who said good morning to the watch, which not many do. You think it's a rum business when the Purveyor's office cares how a clerk gets filed. You're not paid to think, so you've kept that to yourself.
- Assembly duty: the whole watch lines the procession route, and the Captain wants everything else done before the bells, without fuss.
- Bodies: a suicide goes in the pit outside the north gate by sundown, and after that nobody asks again. A murder goes to the cold room at the watch house for the surgeon, and someone has to sign for it. You're not signing for it.
- An order stands until the Captain writes a new one, or the Sarge signs for something and takes it on herself.
