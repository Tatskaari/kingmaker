# The order of procession

- Assembly morning: the procession leaves the palace gate at the ninth bell and winds through the market square to the Great Hall, with the whole watch lining the route. You've timed every step of it, twice.
- The order: the three visiting delegations under their banners, then the Purveyor's party, fourth, with a cart of grain in garlands "for the people's confidence", then the court, and the King last, under Caerwyn's open hand.
- That cart was Master Sallow's idea. A month ago he asked the King, the King agreed, and you redrew the whole order at a day's notice. You have not forgotten it, or who asked.
- A fortnight ago a request came on Purveyor's office paper that Captain Bunce of the watch ride at the head of the route. You filed it under "no".
- An arrest in the long gallery, before the Purveyor's party goes down, costs four minutes. An arrest in the courtyard, in front of the delegations, costs the Assembly. You've worked it out.
