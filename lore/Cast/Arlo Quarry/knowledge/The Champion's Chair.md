# The champion's chair

- The Rat's only armchair, by the fire: worn leather, a sprung seat, and a dip in the cushion the shape of eleven years of Hobb. It's kept for whoever is arm-wrestling champion of the river stairs, and nobody else sits in it. Not even Nan.
- The house custom: elbows on the table, right hands, Nan referees, and wagers are paid at the bar. Anyone may challenge the champion. A champion who refuses three challenges in one night gives up the chair, and it goes to the challenger.
- Before Hobb it was Long Tom Ferris, a coal-heaver, who held it nine years. Hobb took it off him at nineteen, in one go, and Long Tom bought him a pint and never wrestled again.
- Champion of the river stairs: the lightermen would make way for you on the water, and the girl on the stairs would look twice.
- You've wanted it since you were a boy watching from the door.
