# Arlo Quarry

A young lighterman on the river, and a regular at the Drowned Rat.

## Look
Built like a water butt, with his sleeves rolled to show his forearms, which he keeps flexing. A fresh black eye. His elbow is on the nearest table at all times, in case.

## Known for
- Arm-wrestling anyone, for anything. He has challenged Big Hobb more often than anyone on the stairs, and lost to him every time.
- Wagering on everything: "Penny says I'd have you."
- Calling everyone "champ", sarcastically.
- Wanting the champion's chair at the Rat, out loud, most nights.
- His black eye, which he says three Saltmere bargees gave him. Nobody on the stairs has met the bargees.
