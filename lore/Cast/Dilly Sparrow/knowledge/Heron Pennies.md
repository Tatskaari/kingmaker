# Heron pennies

- New silver pennies, bright as a fish, with a little heron stamped where the King's face should be. Nobody on the river had seen one before this month.
- Hobb pays in them, and you've won the best part of three shillings of them off him. Some are on the table in front of you; the rest are in a stocking aboard the Dabchick.
- The heron is Saltmere's bird, and Saltmere puts it on everything. This one is a Saltmere grain factor's mark: it's on the sign of the Heron warehouse at the downriver end of the wharves, and on the sacks that have been going downriver on Saltmere barges this past month, low in the water. So it's Saltmere money, paid out at the Heron.
- Downriver at Saltmere they'd take a heron penny without blinking. In Caerwyn the chandlers want the King's face, and Nan keeps hers in a jar marked FOREIGN.
- Silver's silver, whatever bird is on it. But you don't like money that makes a good lad drink like that.
- On your next run downriver you'll spend them in Saltmere, on brandy.
