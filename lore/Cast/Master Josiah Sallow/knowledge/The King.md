# The King

- King Aldren signs nothing that might upset anyone, and you've made yourself someone it would upset him to lose: this week the feast, the city's bread and the office's goodwill all run through you.
- He remembers how people take their tea. You've always thought that a charming weakness. You've never wondered how he takes his own.
- A month ago you asked that the Purveyor's party walk fourth in the Assembly procession, beside a cart of grain in garlands, "for the people's confidence". He agreed at once, being asked. The Marshal redrew the order of procession with a face like a slammed door.
- What you want from him: to be thanked, in public, for feeding the Assembly in a hungry winter, and the Royal Purveyor's chain when Sir Gilbert goes.
- What you believe that might be wrong: that he'll never act against an officer of the Crown on the eve of the Assembly, or the morning of it, whatever he's told. Given something he can hold, and someone who'll stand behind it in front of him, he might.
- The lever, both ways: "after the Assembly" is his favourite answer, and yours to offer. But he remembers small things, and a clerk who took his tea milk first was a small thing.
