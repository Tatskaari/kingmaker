# Big Hobb

- Hobb Mallory: a dock porter the size of a door, who does your heavy work for double pay in new silver and the honour of being trusted by a gentleman. He calls you "Master". Reliable as furniture, and about as talkative.
- He works nights at the Heron, on the hoist, and has a brass token for its river door.
- He drinks somewhere on the river stairs, as porters do. You have never been down the river stairs and never will.
- In the loft he went to pieces at the wrong moment, and took the skin off his right palm on the hoist rope. You told him to keep the hand out of sight and his mouth shut, and you've told him how patient the river is with men who talk. He has a mother in the Shambles; you mentioned her. Men with mothers are reliable.
- What you believe that might be wrong: he's too frightened of you to talk, and too slow to think of anything else.
- What would change your mind: a porter who panics once will panic again. If he ever runs to you with news, or anyone comes asking after a big man with a bandaged hand, he's a loose end, and the Crakes know the way home along the wharf, by the water.
