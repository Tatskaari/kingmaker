# Master Josiah Sallow

Under-purveyor for grain and flour in the Royal Purveyor's office at the palace, which buys everything the palace eats, drinks and burns, and this year the Centennial Assembly's feast.

## Look
Fifty, pink and well-fed, in a good dark coat with a fur collar. A silver-topped cane, a wheat-ear signet ring (the Purveyor's office's mark) and so much lavender water that you smell him a full minute before you see him.

## Known for
- Signing for the palace's grain: "J. Sallow, for the Royal Purveyor". The Royal Purveyor himself, Sir Gilbert Mothersole, is eighty-seven and seldom seen.
- Getting things done. At the palace he's known as a most obliging gentleman.
- Coming to the King's Granary with the Purveyor's wagons. He has his own key to its big doors.
- A hard season: goblins on the King's Road have cost the office wagon after wagon, and Master Sallow has been very brave about it.
