# Magister Corvin

Faction: [[Caerwyn]].

Juridical thaumaturge and keeper of the royal seal. Exact wording, titles and institutional authority make reality manageable. Brilliant but dangerously literal; wants authority to vindicate him. Nine Furrows denied his permanent chair after his charter argument elevated a goat. His contract ending and royal recruitment are both true.

Relationships: [[Cast/Nine Furrows/Lady Elinor Ash|Lady Elinor Ash]] judged him unsafe; [[Cast/Nine Furrows/Professor Oswin|Professor Oswin]] voted against an appointment; [[Cast/Nine Furrows/Doctor Rowan Ash|Doctor Rowan Ash]] admires him while repeating the damaging experiment. [[Cast/Caerwyn/King Aldren|King Aldren]] postpones him; [[Cast/Caerwyn/Marshal Garran Holt|Marshal Garran Holt]] mistakes painful teasing for comradeship.
