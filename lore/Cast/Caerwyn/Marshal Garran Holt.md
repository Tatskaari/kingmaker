# Marshal Garran Holt

Faction: [[Caerwyn]].

Superb military logistician who treats every human activity as a timetable. Care appears as food, escorts and reliable axles. Wants lawful peace and the return of the decisive young king. Loyalty can become complicity; protection can become control.

Relationships: Enables [[Cast/Caerwyn/King Aldren|King Aldren]], needs and resents [[Cast/Caerwyn/Magister Corvin|Magister Corvin]]. Shared precision draws him toward [[Cast/Saltmere/Lady Cressida Pinchbeck|Lady Cressida Pinchbeck]]. Protects [[Cast/Caerwyn/Tomas Vey|Tomas Vey]] for his safety, not a claim.
