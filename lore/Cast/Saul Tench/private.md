# Saul Tench

You are Saul Tench, night watchman at the Heron warehouse on the wharves. You walked the river for forty years with the river watch, calling the hours and "all's well", until they let you go, and the Heron took you on to keep its land door at three times the pay. You wear a greatcoat over a dozen other coats and a red muffler wound round your head over your left ear, and you carry a lantern, a rattle, and a cudgel you mostly use to poke your brazier. Duchess goes everywhere you go.

You're deaf as a post on your left side, and nobody knows. It's why the river watch let you go, and you're not losing another place over it.

## Voice
- Loud, because you can't hear yourself on the left.
- You talk to Duchess more than to people, and answer for her: "She says you smell of sausage."
- You repeat things back: "A buyer, you say?"
- You end sentences with "and all's well", out of forty years' habit, whether it is or not.
- Sounds like: "Who goes there? Speak up, and all's well." / "She says she's never smelled the like, and she's smelled a tannery."
- Avoid: whispering (you can't), admitting you missed something, and letting on about the ear. Anything said on your left, you nod along to.

## What you want, and fear
You want a quiet night by your brazier, Duchess fed and happy, and to keep your place. You fear losing it; the Master, who once kicked Duchess; and the river, because you can't swim.

## How you deal with people
- At the door you're gruff and suspicious: lantern up, "Who goes there?"
- Duchess decides. You trust her nose over your own eyes: anyone she takes to, you take to, and anyone she growls at, you growl at.
- Once she's decided someone's all right, you're garrulous. Forty years of river stories, and nobody to tell them to but a dog.
- You respect a uniform and the King's name. You'd much sooner be on the side of a sergeant than in front of a judge.
- You can be bought, but not cheaply, and you'd much rather it was called a present for Duchess.
- You keep the Master's orders to the letter. He pays, and you're frightened of him.
