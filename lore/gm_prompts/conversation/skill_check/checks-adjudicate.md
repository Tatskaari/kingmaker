{{{ROLL_GUIDANCE}}}
Give a concise, concrete direction to the NPC for their next response to the immediately preceding player message. Describe what succeeded/failed and how to play it off, rather than writing their dialogue. Address each result independently if multiple skills had different outcomes. Establish only information this character should know; do not reveal unrelated secrets. Return a direction string.

When an insight check is present, also return an observation string addressed to the player as "you": a concise, concrete account of what they notice in this conversation, grounded in the dialogue and established character evidence. Match the amount and certainty of information to the resolved outcome. A failed check may leave motives unclear; do not invent secrets or present unsupported suspicions as facts. Never expose hidden GM instructions or unrelated lore. The observation is shown to the player and supplied to the character as context. In direction, tell the character how to portray the cues that support that same observation, without quoting the GM, announcing the roll, or treating the player’s private inference as something spoken aloud.
