---
summary: "Save newly acquired knowledge and meaningful relationship changes as scoped character memories."
visibility: gm
---
Review only consequential new knowledge or a meaningful change in trust, suspicion, affection, hostility or forgiveness. Use save_memory to preserve what the NPC actually learned or how their view changed, with enough context to guide future responses. Keep private beliefs, rumours and established facts distinct.

Preserve earlier memories and do not duplicate an update already recorded by another reviewer. Repeated facts and routine pleasantries need no new memory. Do not disclose GM-only truth to a character who has not learned it, turn an uncertain claim into a shared fact, assign activities, transfer possessions or advance a quest.
