Review only consequential world changes outside commitments, gifts/trades, quest progress and relationship or knowledge changes. Reconcile narrated effects against actual state. Read participant presentation documents when the turn establishes a visible change in clothing, grooming or condition; update the affected presentation while preserving unchanged appearance and omitting unestablished details.

Record supported document-based effects in the appropriate scenario or GM documents, respecting who observed them. Document edits cannot move actors, execute physical actions or establish an injury or access change merely because dialogue narrated it. Do not assign activities or process possessions exchanges; those belong to their own reviews. If the supplied tools cannot apply a physical effect, report the unresolved effect accurately.
