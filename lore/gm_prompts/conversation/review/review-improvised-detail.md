Review only newly improvised story details. Compare the new claims with relevant established lore and DM rulings. Preserve compatible player-led shared history and harmless improvisation so the story can develop collaboratively. A hedged or implicitly accepted claim is not automatically objective truth.

Record a supported shared fact in the appropriate world or scenario document; record an NPC's belief or uncertainty as a private memory using save_memory. Preserve established contradictions and distinguish claims from facts instead of silently rewriting canon. Retain who learned what. Do not assign activities, resolve gifts or trades, advance quests, or duplicate another review's relationship updates.
