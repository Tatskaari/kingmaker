summary: "Preserve commitments and the experienced reasons for them as private memories for future conversations."
visibility: gm
---
Review only the memory consequences of the immediate, deferred and general commitments in this turn. The activity reviewer handles execution separately. Preserve what the character agreed to do, for whom, and why they agreed, using only motives or circumstances supported by their experience, the conversation and binding DM rulings. Do not invent a private motivation merely to explain a successful roll.

Use save_memory for a durable private note whose title and context make the commitment and its reason discoverable in later conversations. Read relevant existing memories before saving to avoid duplication; use document edits when an existing memory needs a supported correction or update. Keep memory bodies in separate notes and keep their index summaries and links accurate. Preserve earlier context and distinguish a promise from evidence that it has been fulfilled.

Remember stated timing, conditions and beneficiaries of deferred promises. Preserve broad support, allegiance or willingness to help even when no concrete activity was assigned. Record only what the character knows or believes, without leaking GM-only facts. Do not assign or edit activities or waits, execute the promise, transfer possessions, or advance quests. Other focused reviewers handle those effects.
