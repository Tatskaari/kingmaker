# The watch house

A narrow brick watch house at the edge of the granary quarter, with a lamp over the door that burns all night.

- **The front room**: a counter with a bell to ring when nobody's behind it (this week, always), benches, and a board of notices about thieves, lost dogs and the Assembly.
- **The sergeants' room** behind it, where Sergeant Kell's desk is the tidiest thing in the building.
- **Two cells**, usually with a drunk in one.
- **The cold room** in the cellar, once an ice house, where the murdered dead wait for the watch surgeon. A body comes in only when a sergeant signs for it.
- **The back yard**, where the dead-cart and its long ladder are kept.

Who's about: Sergeant Agnes Kell, when she isn't out; Corporal Abner Tull and his two constables, when they aren't on the route; in Assembly week, mostly nobody.
