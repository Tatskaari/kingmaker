# The palace kitchens

Vaulted kitchens off the kitchen yard in the palace's back courts: spits, bread ovens, a pastry room, cold larders, a scullery, and more heat, steam and shouting than anywhere else in Caerwyn. On the eve of the Assembly they're cooking the feast for four delegations.

- **Mistress Pruett**, the palace cook, runs them like a warship and hears everything.
- **Deliveries** come from the Royal Purveyor's office through the service entrance and the kitchen yard. The kitchens have opinions about them, about under-purveyors, and about purse silver stamped with a wheat-ear so that the kitchens can't pinch it.
- **Gossip** comes up from the river with the eels and the fish, and goes out again with the scraps.
- **Extra hands**: on a feast day the kitchens take anyone who'll scrub, turn a spit or carry a bucket, and feed them. Nobody asks a child with a bucket who they are.
