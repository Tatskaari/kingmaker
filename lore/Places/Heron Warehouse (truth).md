# The Heron warehouse: the truth

This is where the King's grain goes. The wagons written off as "lost to goblins" come here by night, and the grain is re-sacked under the heron mark and sold downriver as Saltmere grain. The warehouse buys it from Sallow knowing whose it is.

- **The true book**: the warehouse's own ledger of every "lost" royal wagon it bought, re-sacked and sold. This is the proof.
- **Sallow** comes at night to see a barge loaded.
- **The river door** opens to Big Hobb's brass token.
- **Heron pennies**: the warehouse's Saltmere buyers pay in new silver pennies stamped with a heron, and Sallow pays the goblins of the burnt mill with them.
- **Ways in**: sneaking, bluffing as a buyer, bribing the night watch, Hobb's token, or a diversion.
- **Dangers**: the river in spate, the hoists, and Sallow's men.
