# The Drowned Rat

A dockside dive on a landing halfway down the river stairs: low beams, river damp and a fire that smokes. It's where the dock-porters, bargemen and carters drink.

- **Nan Quill**, the landlady, keeps a cudgel called Manners and sells gossip by the measure, like gin. She prices it by how much trouble it'll cause.
- **The house rules** are posted by the door and enforced, by Nan and by Manners. Breaking one costs, and fighting costs most.
- **What's on**: ale, gin, cards, and arm-wrestling for drinks or money.
- **The regulars**: dock-porters and bargemen, and every night Big Hobb Mallory, a dock-porter the size of a door.
