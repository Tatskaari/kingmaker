# The Drowned Rat: the truth

**Big Hobb Mallory** is Sallow's muscle, and the big man with the bandaged hand. The bandage on his right hand covers a rope burn from hauling Prout up on the sack hoist. He drinks here every night, and more than usual since. He isn't a killer by nature: he's frightened of Sallow, frightened of what he did, and frightened of the hand being noticed, so he keeps it in his lap or wrapped round his tankard.

What he knows and has:
- What happened in the tally loft: he and Sallow killed Prout and hung him up to look like a suicide.
- The Heron warehouse: whose grain goes through it, and its night routine, including when Sallow comes to see a barge loaded.
- A brass token that opens the warehouse's river door.

Ways to get it out of him: drink, cards, arm-wrestling, Nan's gossip by the measure, fear, kindness, a trick, or a fight (Nan's house rules make fights costly).

**Nan Quill** knows everything said under her roof and sells it by the measure. She has noticed Hobb drinking harder and watching the door since his hand went into a bandage.
