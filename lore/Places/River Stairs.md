# The river stairs and the docks

The river stairs run from the bottom of the town down the steep bank to the wharves: worn stone steps, wet all winter, with landings where porters wait for work with their hooks and coils of rope. Nobody has ever counted the same number of steps twice.

- **The Drowned Rat** sits on a landing halfway down.
- **The wharves** run along the river at the foot of the stairs. The warehouses have hoists jutting out over the water and river doors at water level, so barges can load straight from the store. The grain warehouses are at the downriver end, and the Heron warehouse is the last of them.
- **The river** is in winter spate: brown, fast and freezing. What goes in comes out at the sea, or doesn't.
- **Who's about**: dock-porters (big men, paid by the load, who drink it by the night), bargemen, eel-sellers, Saltmere factors' clerks, and mudlarks picking the mud for anything worth a penny.
- **After dark** the wharves are black and quiet but for the odd barge loading by lantern. The watch comes down only when it must.
