# The Heron warehouse

The last warehouse at the downriver end of the wharves, under a painted heron sign: the house mark of a Saltmere grain factor. Sacks go out of it marked with the heron.

- **The river door** at water level, where barges tie up and load straight from the store.
- **Hoists** jutting out over the water.
- **A night watchman**, with a lantern and opinions about loiterers.

Dock talk: the Heron has been busy at night this past month, sending barges downriver low in the water while bread in Caerwyn is dear. It pays porters well for night loading, and doesn't care for questions.
