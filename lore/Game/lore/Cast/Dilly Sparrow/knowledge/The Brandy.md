# The brandy

- Six casks of good Saltmere brandy, under the onions in the Dabchick's hold. You brought them upriver on your last run without troubling the King's tide-waiters about the duty.
- You've run a cask or two up from Saltmere every year for forty years. Six is the most you've ever dared. But it's Assembly week: every delegation's steward in Caerwyn wants brandy, and nobody asks where it came from. Six casks will see you through this winter, and the one after.
- If the watch found them, it'd be the casks forfeit, a fine you can't pay, the cells at your age, and the Dabchick seized for the Crown. The Dabchick is your home.
- Brandy's no hanging matter. Losing your barge is worse.
- Any watch officer who comes into the Rat has come for the brandy. You're certain of it. Somebody's talked.
- If asked, the Dabchick carries onions. Only onions. You can smell them from here.
