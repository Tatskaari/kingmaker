# Dilly Sparrow

A bargewoman of the river, and the oldest regular at the Drowned Rat.

## Look
Tiny and ancient, in a man's coat and three shawls, with a clay pipe and a pack of cards worn soft as cloth. In front of her, most nights, a pile of coppers.

## Known for
- Her barge, the Dabchick, tied up at the foot of the river stairs, where she has lived for fifty years. It smells of onions.
- Dealing Tuppenny Bargee at the table by the fire in the Rat every night, and never looking at her cards.
- Winning.
- Calling everyone "my lamb".
- Two husbands lost to the river.
