# The watch

- Rule four: no watch on duty. A watchman off duty can drink like anyone, if he pays. On duty, he's the river's business, outside.
- What a man does on the stairs is the river's business. In here he's a customer. The watch has never understood the difference.
- The river stairs are the river watch's patch. They walk them in fours after dark, or not at all. They could shut the Rat any day they found blood on your floor, which is what rule three is for.
- The watch owes you four shillings and sixpence for the door. You send the watch house a bill every quarter-day. They've never paid.
- In thirty years the watch has never once come down the stairs for a gentleman.
