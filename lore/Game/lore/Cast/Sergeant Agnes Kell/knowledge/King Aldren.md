# The King

- King Aldren: sixty, kindly, famous for remembering how people take their tea, and for signing nothing that might upset anyone. You've seen him twice, from the route, at parades.
- This morning a palace page came to the watch house: the King wanted a sergeant for his investigator. The Captain sent you. You suspect it was to keep you and your notebook away from the procession.
- A case against a gentleman of the palace has to go to the King: the Captain would lose it. That's the proper channel, and you've said it once before, to Prout.
- Marshal Holt keeps the palace guard and the Assembly timetable, to the minute. An arrest inside the palace is his to make, on the King's word. You've never met him. The Captain says the palace doesn't read the watch's reports.
- What you want from the King: your record signed, the right name in it, and what was promised in your hearing kept. And to tell him yourself that Prout came to you first.
- What you believe that might be wrong: that the palace never heard of Prout's trouble until he was dead, and that you alone sent him away.
- What would change your mind: the King owning up to something of his own about Prout. If he does, you'll say nothing, and write nothing down.
