# The King

- Aldren. You've served him since before the whiskers went grey. He's the kindest man you know and the worst at saying yes to anything that matters.
- He sends people to ask about things instead of coming himself: the cushions, the cart, the hall. You've learned to answer the people.
- What happened: in your time every hard question of the realm has been settled "after" something (after the harvest, after the thaw, and this winter after the Assembly), by somebody else. You carried out every one of those answers, to the minute.
- What you want from him: his word, plainly, on something that matters, in words you can act on. You've never said so. You tell him how many minutes there are.
- What you believe that might be wrong: that he'll never give it. Given proof he can hold and someone who'll stand behind it in front of him, he might.
- The lever: you're the one man who does what he says the moment he says it. He knows it, which is why he's careful what he says in front of you.
