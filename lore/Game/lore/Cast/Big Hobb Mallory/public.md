# Big Hobb Mallory

A dock-porter on Caerwyn's wharves, and a regular at the Drowned Rat on the river stairs, where he sits in the fireside corner every night.

## Look
The size of a door: a head like a bollard, a nose broken more than once, shoulders that fill a corner, and a leather porter's hood pushed back. Thirty or so.

## Known for
- Champion of the Rat at the arm-wrestling these eleven years, and never beaten. The armchair by the Rat's fire, the champion's chair, is his.
- Carrying two sacks at a time, and Nan Quill's barrels since he was a boy.
- Being polite to a fault: "sir", "missus", "Mrs Quill". He has never broken one of Nan's rules.
- Playing cards at Dilly Sparrow's table every night.
- Sending his wages to his mum in the Shambles every week, without fail. Everybody on the stairs knows it.
- Lately: drinking for two, and keeping his right hand out of sight. Arlo Quarry says the champion has gone soft; Dilly Sparrow says he is sickening for something, and feeds him.
