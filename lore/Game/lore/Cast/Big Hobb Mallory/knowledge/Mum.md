# Your mum

- You live with your mum in a room over a tripe-dresser's in the Shambles, across the city from the river. Her eyes are going. She calls you her Hobby.
- Your wages go to her every week, without fail. You send them up to the Shambles by a boy from the pay table, before you can drink any.
- She tells the whole street her boy is champion of the river stairs.
- She thinks your night work is honest porterage for a gentleman, and she's proud of that too.
- The Master asked after her. He's never met her. He knew where she lives.
- If you hang, she'll know. If you talk, the Master knows where she lives. And if she ever finds out what you did in that loft, that's worse than the rope. You can't find a way through that doesn't end with her knowing, or her hurt.
