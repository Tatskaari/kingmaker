# Big Hobb

- He works the hoist on loading nights: strong as two men, slow, polite, never any trouble. He isn't one of yours.
- He's the Master's man. He walks at the Master's heel on the wharf and takes the Master's extra silver. You don't talk in front of him, and the gang don't either. He doesn't know about the skim.
- Whatever else the Master has him do, you don't want to know.
- You heard about the clerk found hanged at the granary, like everyone on the wharf. You've thought about it, and stopped.
