# Ledger page

A page razored from the Royal Granary Roll: wagons 'lost to goblins', countersigned 'J. Sallow, for the Royal Purveyor', with Prout's pencilled river-warehouse weights and 'Not goblins. Who pays the hedge-folk?'

## Full discovery

Sewn into the lining of Prout's left boot was a page razored from the Royal Granary Roll: a dozen grain wagons for the King's Road this past month marked 'lost to goblins', each countersigned 'J. Sallow, for the Royal Purveyor', with Prout's pencilled note beside each of what the same wagon weighed at the river warehouses two days later, and at the foot: 'Not goblins. Who pays the hedge-folk?'

## Starting location

Sewn into the lining of Prout's left boot. Cob is wearing the boots. The authored starting tags are **evidence, hidden**.
