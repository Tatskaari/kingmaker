# Kingmaker lore

Caerwyn on the eve of the Centennial Assembly, for the arc `hanged-clerk` → `drowned-rat` → `heron-warehouse` → `kings-morning`.

- [World](World/index.md) — the city and court of Caerwyn, how the town stands right now, the royal palace, the King's Road, the City Watch, the Royal Purveyor's office, the Grain Crisis and the Centennial Assembly, and what's in motion.
- [Places](Places/index.md) — the granary yard, the watch house, the market square and the Corn Exchange, the river stairs and docks, the Drowned Rat, the Heron warehouse and the palace kitchens.
- [Cast](Cast/index.md) — the people: who they are, what they know of one another, and what they remember.

## How the lore is kept

- **Public** docs hold what anyone in the story could know or see. **Private** docs are read only by the characters in their `readers`. **Gm** docs are the GM's own truth.
- A public doc's secrets go in a gm doc beside it, named "<doc> (truth).md". The public doc links to it with a label that says when to read it, never what it says, because characters can read the public doc and its links.
- Entry docs end with "## Read when relevant": labelled links to deeper docs, opened only when play goes there.
- Links are relative Markdown links. In the path, write spaces as %20 and brackets as %28 and %29.
- **One home per fact.** A world fact lives in the world or place doc it belongs to, not only in a scene's prose, and anything that needs it links to it or cites it. For example, the delegations' banners are in the Centennial Assembly doc, which is why the heron stays one Saltmere factor's mark and never becomes Saltmere's.

## How the world moves

- **What's in motion** ([World/Fronts.md](World/Fronts.md), gm): each front has someone behind it, a want, a clock of steps (each with an "if" or a time, and how it shows), a doom, stakes questions and the scene endings that tick it. Steps tick at scene boundaries, from what play did and from time, never because it would be dramatic. A step that play has made meaningless is struck out, with the reason.
- **How the town stands now** ([World/Caerwyn now.md](World/Caerwyn%20now.md), public): what anyone could see or hear, hour by hour, each piece of talk with the people who say it. A step that ticks shows there once, as something seen or someone who'd say it.
- **Scenes read the world rather than restating it.** A scene's `lore` lists its place doc and Caerwyn now. Its setting shows one detail of the town's troubles as they look in that place. Its limits cite a world rule and its keeper. Its cards read front positions ("if the Captain told him...") without assuming them, and its exits say which fronts they tick.
- **Reviewers record a change where it lives.** Replace a doc's "What's happening now" rather than adding to it, and keep a short note of what happened. Say who did it, who saw it, who gains and who's hurt. Tick or strike front steps that play made true or meaningless, never for drama, and never answer a stakes question. Public talk goes in Caerwyn now with its mouth, and truths stay in gm docs. A bill that comes due later is an entry in Fronts, with an "if" and the place it will show. When "What's happening now" changes, update the doc's summary to match.
