# Succession and Responsibility

[[King Aldren]] avoids frightening decisions through trivial brilliance; [[Magister Corvin]] wants law to matter but also wants vindication; [[Marshal Garran Holt]] keeps the system functioning while enabling its failures. The succession crisis exceeds what their arrangement can contain. The player should be able to use their competence without endorsing their obsessions.
