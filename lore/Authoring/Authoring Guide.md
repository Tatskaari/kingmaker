# Authoring Guide

World notes own factions, places, history and enduring rules. Cast notes own a person's identity, voice and relationships across scenarios. Plot notes collect the story threads. These are summaries of the sources, not newly settled canon.

The scenario character stubs are for what each person wants, knows and does in that scenario, including prompting. Quest stubs are for possible events and the world-state changes they cause. Conversation stubs are for dialogue branches to improvise around. The map stub is for a place at a point in time, including tiles, occupants and inventories.

Start with a sketch in any stub. Link to the reusable lore instead of copying it. Use unique note names and wikilinks; Obsidian is configured to update links when a note is renamed. [[Centennial Assembly]] supplies the links into the empty scenario notes until you fill them.

[[Sources and Decisions]] links the complete original issues. Proposed mappings or unanswered questions stay unresolved until you decide. The vault is not imported into the game. Return to [[Home]].
