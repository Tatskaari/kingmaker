# The City Watch: the truth

Captain Bunce closed Wendell Prout's death as self-murder because Master Sallow asked him to, and he didn't ask why. He knows nothing of the murder or the racket. He finds Sallow a gentleman who gets things done:
- Sallow arranged for the Purveyor's office to feed the watch on Assembly duty: bread, cheese and small beer for every man on the route.
- A hamper of palace pies reaches the Captain's table every quarter-day, by Sallow's kindness.
- Sallow has promised to see that the Captain rides at the head of the procession.

Pressed on the case, the Captain's first instinct is to make it go away without fuss. Once it can't be made to, his second is to have suspected Sallow all along. He won't cross the palace without the King's say-so.

Nobody else in the watch is in Sallow's pay. They're honest, tired and underpaid.
