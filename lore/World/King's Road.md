# The King's Road

Caerwyn's main road, and its grain road: the palace's grain wagons, a wheat-ear on their canvas, come in along it from the Nine Furrows farms. Safe passage depends on the Marshal's patrols, and this week they've all been called into the city to line the Assembly route.

Going out from the city:
- **Garrow's toll bridge**, a few hours' walk out: a humpbacked stone bridge over the river, the only crossing for miles. Garrow takes the King's penny (a silver penny a head), the Treasury's Assembly levy and the Marshal's two pennies a weapon, and writes everyone in his ledger.
- **The crossroads**, a mile further: a milestone, an empty gibbet and blackthorn hedges. The drovers' track leaves here for the Nine Furrows farmlands (Hollis Farm, Harrowby).
- **The burnt mill**, on the river half a day north of the crossroads. Goblins live under it, people say.

**Goblins.** The hedge-goblins have been bolder this winter than anyone can remember: oxen shot with black-fletched arrows, travellers chased, and a dozen of the palace's grain wagons lost this past month. Goblins are small, green and quick, call people "longlegs" and love anything shiny.
