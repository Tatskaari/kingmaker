# The King's Road

Caerwyn's main road, and its grain road: the palace's grain wagons come in along it from the Nine Furrows farms under wheat-ear canvas. You notice the ruts, the Assembly traffic, and how everyone on it means to be off it before dark.

**What's here, going out from the city**
- **Garrow's toll bridge**, a few hours' walk out: a humpbacked stone bridge over the river, the only crossing for miles, with a whitewashed toll house at the near end.
- **The crossroads**, a mile further: a milestone, an empty gibbet and blackthorn hedges. The drovers' track leaves here for the Nine Furrows farmlands (Hollis Farm, Harrowby).
- **The burnt mill**, on the river half a day north of the crossroads. Goblins live under it, people say.

**Who's about, and why**
- Carters and drovers bringing grain and beasts in for the Assembly; travellers and delegations' baggage trains going to it.
- The palace's grain wagons with the Purveyor's carters, and the office's under-purveyor for grain riding out with them.
- Garrow, the toll-warden, with his ledger.
- The hedge-goblins: small, green and quick. They call people "longlegs" and love anything shiny.

**What it needs, trades and resents**: it needs patrols, and hasn't got any. It carries grain into the city and Assembly money out of travellers' purses. Carters resent paying the arms order on a road where goblins shoot oxen.

**The rules of the road, and who keeps them**
- **Safe passage** depends on Marshal Holt's patrols, and this week they've all been called into the city to line the Assembly route.
- **At the bridge** Garrow takes the King's penny (a silver penny a head), the Treasury's Assembly levy and the Marshal's two pennies a weapon, and writes everyone in his ledger.

**How the town's troubles show here**: the hedge-goblins have been bolder this winter than anyone can remember. Oxen have been shot with black-fletched arrows, travellers chased, and a dozen of the palace's grain wagons lost this past month. Carters won't take the road after dark without a patrol, and there are no patrols.

**What people say, and who says it**
- Carters: no patrols, no night driving.
- Farmers from the drovers' track: they've never seen goblins carry off a whole wagon of wheat, and wonder where a goblin would sell one.

**What's happening now**: Assembly traffic on the road by day, and nobody on it after dark.
