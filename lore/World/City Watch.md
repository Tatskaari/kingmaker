# The City Watch

The watch keeps the King's peace in Caerwyn's streets: a Captain, a handful of sergeants, corporals and constables, and never enough of any of them. Rain capes and truncheons, and notebooks for the sergeants. They walk in pairs after dark, and go down the river stairs at night only when they must.

- **The Captain**, Aubrey Bunce: a fine figure on a horse, fond of a parade, the palace and a quiet life. His favourite words are "without fuss". This week he's at the palace, seeing to the procession route.
- **The Assembly route**: the whole watch is out lining it, hanging bunting, moving carts and chasing pigs. Until after the Assembly nobody can be spared for anything else.
- **The dead-cart**: a handcart with a long ladder lashed on top, pushed by two constables. It collects the city's dead.
- **The Captain's book**: every death in the city goes in it as natural, accident, self-murder or murder, and the Captain's word stands. A sergeant may sign a body in as a murder instead, and answers to the Captain for it.
- **A murder** goes to the cold room at the watch house, to wait for the watch surgeon (on duty at the palace this week).
- **A suicide** goes into the pit outside the north gate, under quicklime, by sundown on the day it's found. No grave, no stone, and after that nobody asks again.
- **What the watch may not do**: lay hands on an officer of the royal household, the Purveyor's office included, without a warrant under the royal seal, unless they're caught in the act.
