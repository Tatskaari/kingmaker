# The Centennial Assembly

Every hundred years the kingdoms must publicly recognise one common sovereign. This winter the Assembly meets at King Aldren's palace in Caerwyn. It opens with a procession on the morning after the eve; then the Assembly sits in the Great Hall, and there is a feast.

The delegations, all arrived:
- **Caerwyn**, the host: roads, courts and garrisons.
- **Nine Furrows**: wizards of the ancient university, who see to weather, wards and grain.
- **Kläggenheim**: dwarven miners and engineers, and old King Gurt.
- **Saltmere**: ships, warehouses, insurance and credit, and Prince Peregrine.

The arrangements:
- **The procession** leaves the palace gate and winds through the city by way of the market square. The whole watch is out lining the route.
- **The feast** is the Royal Purveyor's office's to supply, grain crisis or no grain crisis.
- **The Treasury's Assembly levy**: a shilling a head, "to defray the costs of the Centennial", collected from "such travellers as are able and willing".
- **Marshal Holt's arms order**: two pennies for each weapon borne on the King's Road in Assembly week. Weapons peace-bonded with red cord and wax go free.

Nothing else gets done this week. Every official is at the palace, and the answer to every request is "After the Assembly."
