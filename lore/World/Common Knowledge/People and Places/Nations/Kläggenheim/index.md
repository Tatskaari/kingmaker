# Kläggenheim

Kläggenheim is a dwarven kingdom known for mines, machinery and well-equipped soldiers. It has more mouths than farmland; grain arriving from abroad matters as much as ore coming out of the mountains.

Formal dwarven names record ancestry, crafts and old grievances. Gurt, Klog and Bran are the short names used among humans; they sound distinctly undignified to dwarven ears.
