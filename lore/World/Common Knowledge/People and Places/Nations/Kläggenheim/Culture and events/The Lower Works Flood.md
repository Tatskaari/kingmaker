# The Lower Works Flood

A failed drainage channel once flooded the lower workshops of [[World/Common Knowledge/People and Places/Nations/Kläggenheim/Cities/Bellowsdeep|Bellowsdeep]]. Water ruined tools, stopped furnaces and left whole shifts without wages.

Bran made his reputation helping get the works dry again. Klog became known for pursuing the smaller claims after the owners had settled their own losses. Both accounts are true; commemorative speeches tend to favour whichever man was invited.

The pumps remain in use. Some compensation claims remain open.
