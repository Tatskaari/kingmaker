# Bellowsdeep

Bellowsdeep is a city of foundries, machine shops and warm stone streets. Shift bells matter more than sunrise, and visitors quickly learn not to lean on an unfamiliar pipe.

Its workshops build pumps and mill machinery for customers far beyond Kläggenheim. The [[World/Common Knowledge/People and Places/Nations/Kläggenheim/Culture and events/The Lower Works Flood|Lower Works Flood]] is still remembered whenever someone proposes saving money on drainage.
