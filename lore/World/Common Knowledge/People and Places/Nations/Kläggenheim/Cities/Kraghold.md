# Kraghold

Kraghold climbs the inside of a mountain in broad stone terraces. The royal halls occupy the oldest workings; newer offices have spread into passages once used to haul ore.

It is the home of the Office of Unsettled Claims. Families come here to register inheritances, dispute boundaries and discover that a dispute was already opened by a great-grandparent. There are excellent inns for people expecting a short visit.
