# Barleygate

Barleygate stands where the mountain road reaches land broad enough for wagon yards. Its great warehouses hold imported grain; its mills turn it into flour before the harder journey uphill.

Merchants from abroad are an ordinary sight here. So are arguments about weights, damp sacks and whose wagon arrived first. The city’s name is much greener than the surrounding countryside.
