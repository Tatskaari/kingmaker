# King Gurt

Gurt is the ancient king of Kläggenheim. Early in his reign, a severe winter left grain wagons stranded below the mountain roads. He had royal storehouses opened to feed households whose own supplies had run out.

In [[World/Common Knowledge/People and Places/Nations/Kläggenheim/Cities/Barleygate|Barleygate]], he is still remembered as the king who asked how much bread the stores would make before asking what the grain was worth.
