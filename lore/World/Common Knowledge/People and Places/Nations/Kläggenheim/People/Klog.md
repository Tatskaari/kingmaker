# Klog

Klog is a senior representative of the Office of Unsettled Claims in [[World/Common Knowledge/People and Places/Nations/Kläggenheim/Cities/Kraghold|Kraghold]]. He began as a clerk copying other people’s petitions and became known for following up cases everyone else considered too small to bother with.

His work after [[World/Common Knowledge/People and Places/Nations/Kläggenheim/Culture and events/The Lower Works Flood|the Lower Works Flood]] helped establish his reputation: if an agreement leaves somebody unpaid, Klog is likely to remember their name.
