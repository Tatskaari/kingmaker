# Caerwyn

A river city of roads, courts and garrisons, capital of the kingdom of Caerwyn, and this winter host to the Centennial Assembly.

**The court.** King Aldren rules from the palace on the rise above the river. He is warm and hospitable, remembers how everyone takes their tea, and is famously unwilling to sign anything that might upset anyone. Marshal Garran Holt keeps the roads, the garrisons and the Assembly timetable, to the minute. Magister Corvin keeps the royal seal and the exact wording of the law.

**The city.**
- The palace on the rise, with its offices and kitchens in the back courts.
- The market square: stalls, the procession route, and the Corn Exchange, where grain factors trade, post prices and keep the wagon lists.
- The granary quarter: the King's Granary, warehouses, porters' lodgings and a watch house.
- The river stairs, down to the docks and the wharves.
- The north gate, where the city keeps what it would rather not look at: the Mending House inside it, the suicides' pit outside it.

**Everyday things.**
- Money: twelve silver pennies to the shilling, five shillings to the crown. A porter earns about eight pennies a day, so a silver crown is more than a week's wages.
- The palace bells ring the hours and the half hours for the whole city.
- Magic is Nine Furrows business: weather, wards and grain. Nobody in Caerwyn talks to the dead, and the dead stay dead, which people find a comfort.
- The Mending House takes in strays and wayward children and mends them with porridge, hymns and picking oakum. Every granary rat would sooner take their chances with the goblins.
- The law: the City Watch keeps the King's peace in the streets, but officers of the royal household answer to the palace. The watch may not lay hands on one without a warrant under the royal seal, unless they're caught in the act.

**The eve of the Assembly**: bunting on every rope that will hold it, the watch out lining the procession route, delegations' servants everywhere, and prices up, bread most of all.
