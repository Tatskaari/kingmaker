# The Grain Crisis: the truth

There are goblins on the King's Road, but they aren't what's taking the wagons.

## The racket

- **Who**: Master Josiah Sallow, under-purveyor for grain in the Royal Purveyor's office. A good coat, a silver-topped cane, a wheat-ear signet ring and too much lavender water. His muscle is Big Hobb Mallory, a dock-porter the size of a door, with some hired men at the warehouse.
- **How**: palace grain wagons on the King's Road are written off in the Royal Granary Roll as "lost to goblins", each countersigned "J. Sallow, for the Royal Purveyor". The grain goes by night to the Heron warehouse on the river, where it is re-sacked under the heron mark of a Saltmere grain factor and sold downriver. The warehouse's own ledger of it all is the true book.
- **The goblins**: Sallow pays the hedge-goblins of the burnt mill to be seen on the road and to raid now and then, so the story holds. He pays in new silver pennies stamped with a heron, which come from the warehouse's Saltmere buyers. To the goblins he's a longlegs in a grey hood who smells of lavender.
- **Why**: money, and standing. An Assembly short of grain and money is one the Purveyor's office gets to save, and Sallow means to be the man who saves it.
- **The Saltmere courier** (background): in the same grey hood, Sallow paid Chief Gubbin to snatch Hester Quayle, the Saltmere courier, at a ford on the King's Road. Her sealed letter is Saltmere's credit for the King's treasury: the loan that pays for the Assembly.

## Prout

Wendell Prout, under-clerk of the Granary Roll, noticed that the "lost" wagons weighed in at the river warehouses two days after each loss. He razored the page out of the Roll, sewed it into his boot and asked Sergeant Kell how a clerk reports false entries. In the small hours of the eve of the Assembly, Sallow and Hobb caught him in the tally loft, killed him and hoisted him on the sack hoist to look like a suicide. At first light Sallow paid a granary girl to say she'd seen him jump, then asked the Watch Captain to close it quietly.

## Who knows what

- **Sallow**: all of it. **Hobb**: the murder, the wagons and the warehouse's night routine. He carries a brass token for its river door.
- **The Heron warehouse's people**: whose grain they're buying. **Gubbin**: a grey hood, lavender and heron pennies, but no name.
- **Cob** heard the murder from his nest in the loft. **Moll** took Sallow's silver and heard his name. **Kell** knows Prout came to her. **The Captain** knows only that Sallow wanted it closed quietly.

## The proof

Prout's page; the Roll, chained to the tally desk; Moll's wheat-ear crown; heron pennies; Hobb's token and his word; and the true book, which would hang Sallow.
