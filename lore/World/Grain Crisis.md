# The Grain Crisis

The harvest was poor, the Nine Furrows granaries are thin, and bread in Caerwyn has gone up a penny a loaf since the autumn.

On top of that, the palace's own grain has been going missing. This past month a dozen wagons bound for the King's Granary along the King's Road have been "lost to goblins". Palace grain lost on the road is written off in the Royal Granary Roll, and the write-off is countersigned by the Royal Purveyor's office, which buys the palace's grain. The office says the Assembly feast will be fed regardless, and that it has the matter in hand.

What people say:
- "Lost to goblins" is what Caerwyn says now about anything that goes missing: a hat, a husband, the rent. It stopped being funny about a fortnight ago.
- Carters won't take the King's Road after dark without a patrol, and there are no patrols.
- Farmers have never seen goblins carry off a whole wagon of wheat, and wonder where a goblin would sell one.
- On the docks they grumble that Saltmere barges are taking grain downriver while Caerwyn goes short.
