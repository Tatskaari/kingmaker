# Nine Furrows

The Ancient and Collegiate University of the Nine Furrows operates Greenweald's granaries, hospitals, irrigation, estates and weather wards. Its exact constitutional relationship to Greenweald remains unresolved.

Delegation: [[Cast/Nine Furrows/Lady Elinor Ash|Lady Elinor Ash]], [[Cast/Nine Furrows/Professor Oswin|Professor Oswin]], [[Cast/Nine Furrows/Doctor Rowan Ash|Doctor Rowan Ash]]. Former academic: [[Cast/Caerwyn/Magister Corvin|Magister Corvin]]. Covenant, ancient agriculture and experimental agrimancy create incompatible but useful expertise. Rival wizards unite against outside criticism; titles are technically defensible weapons of status.
