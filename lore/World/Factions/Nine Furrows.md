# Nine Furrows

The Ancient and Collegiate University of the Nine Furrows operates Greenweald's granaries, hospitals, irrigation, estates and weather wards. Its exact constitutional relationship to Greenweald remains unresolved.

Delegation: [[Lady Elinor Ash]], [[Professor Oswin]], [[Doctor Rowan Ash]]. Former academic: [[Magister Corvin]]. Covenant, ancient agriculture and experimental agrimancy create incompatible but useful expertise. Rival wizards unite against outside criticism; titles are technically defensible weapons of status.

Source: #108 in [[Sources and Decisions]].
