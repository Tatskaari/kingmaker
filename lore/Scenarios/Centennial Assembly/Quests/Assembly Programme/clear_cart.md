# Clear the cart

## Required host effects

Successful remedies converge on the clearance transition. Authored examples include repair with Corvin's book, Rowan backing out and a successful physical smash, but a completed player-devised solution can invoke the same transition when its intended mechanism is established in play. Repair requires the actual book in the player's inventory when it is presented to Rowan. The host clears the cart obstruction and gives every character one fixed private memory that the entrance is clear and deliveries can resume. This public milestone is shared regardless of distance; private negotiations are not broadcast.

Queue a plot review for each character to update their current conversation goals and future branches. Repair leaves Rowan and Nine Furrows pleased; backing out leaves Rowan slightly resentful and Nine Furrows neutral; smashing displeases Nine Furrows even with Holt's permission. For another remedy, infer reactions only from established events and scoped character knowledge. Holt appreciates the cleared entrance. Aldren can acknowledge progress but must not claim his cushions have arrived.

Failed attempts do not clear the cart. Repeated completion does not duplicate memories. Delivery and helper assignments remain separate, unfinished follow-up work; clearance does not move the waiting goods.
