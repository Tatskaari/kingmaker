# Delivery delayed

Rowan's self-guiding cart, carrying a gift tree from Nine Furrows, has wedged itself across the palace service entrance while protecting its cargo. The cushion delivery is queued outside behind it.

Holt has already cleared space twice for Rowan to work. Both times Rowan said he was nearly finished. Holt has now ordered dismantling, but Rowan is physically in the way.
