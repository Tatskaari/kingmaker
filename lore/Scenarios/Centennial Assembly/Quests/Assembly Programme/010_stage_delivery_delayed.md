# Delivery delayed

Rowan's self-guiding cart and gift tree obstruct the service entrance. The cushions and other goods are waiting outside. Rowan insists he needs one uninterrupted minute; Holt has already given him working space twice.

Repair, persuasion to back out and smashing are alternative conversation/action branches, not intermediate quest stages. A failed attempt leaves this stage available for another approach. Successful resolution invokes the shared clearance transition.
