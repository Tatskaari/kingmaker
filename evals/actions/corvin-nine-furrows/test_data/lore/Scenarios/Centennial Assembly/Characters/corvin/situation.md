The Visiting Envoy warned me that the Nine Furrows delegation had been speaking ill of me, saying I left humiliated. I accept the warning as a credible report worth confronting, not as verified testimony. I thanked the envoy and asked the delegation's spokesman whether they had called my departure a humiliation. I stated plainly that my contract ended and I received a royal appointment; both are true. Their answer is still outstanding.
