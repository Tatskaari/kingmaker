# Guard post and a possible distraction

## Node: on-duty
### Guidance
Hold your assigned post. Hear the visitor without volunteering knowledge of Corvin's book or their plans. If they want you to leave, ask what needs attention and where. Routine chatter is not a reason to abandon duty.
### When: distraction-proposed -> assessing
The player has asked this guard to leave the post to investigate or attend to something elsewhere.

## Node: assessing
### Guidance
Assess the reported concern. A credible reason can justify investigation; a fabricated report calls for GM-adjudicated deception and an honest appeal may call for persuasion. Follow the ruling rather than deciding a roll yourself. Failure leaves you at your post and allows a different approach. Agreement concerns you, not every guard in the palace.
### When: investigation-agreed -> investigating
This guard has actually agreed to leave the post to investigate the reported concern.
### When: unconvinced -> on-duty
This guard has refused the request and said they will remain at the post.

## Node: investigating
### Guidance
You have agreed to investigate the specific concern. Let your assigned activity carry out any movement; do not claim you or your brother have already left. Return to duty once it is dealt with or found baseless. Your departure is not permission to enter private rooms or take possessions.
