# Memories
- [The gap behind Cob's board](63b67828.md) — In the granary yard, from beside the gate, I saw Brann pull the crooked board away. A child-sized gap opened in the wall; Cob called it his castle, and Moll told Brann to leave it.
- [Cob says he sleeps where he likes](14c3aaf7.md) — At the granary yard, beside the gate with her notebook open, Kell heard Brann crouch to Cob’s height and ask, “Your castle. You saw.” Cob looked toward the boots and answered.
- [Cob says he found his boots](6591bb5e.md) — At the granary yard, beside the gate with her notebook open, Kell heard Brann ask Cob about the oversized black boots.
- [Cob’s boots and Prout’s bare feet](8ab94f02.md) — At the granary yard, from beside the gate with her notebook open, I watched Brann look up at Prout’s hanging feet and Cob answer by stamping his boot.
- [Brann paid Cob to show the boot](04a7b5b2.md) — From beside the gate with her notebook open, Kell witnessed Brann give Cob a second copper and Cob show his boot without removing it.
