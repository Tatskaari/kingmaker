# Memories
- [Brann found Cob's castle](a8a76579.md) — I was on the wall when Brann pulled the crooked board from the granary wall; I swung down into the yard and warned him off after Cob named the gap.
- [Cob answers Brann by the gap](b545c8c7.md) — From the wall in the granary yard, Moll heard Brann crouch to Cob’s height and speak to him at the crooked-board gap; she watched Cob edge toward the water butt.
- [Cob says he found his boots](1d8bf2c8.md) — In the granary yard, standing closer to Brann and watching Cob, Moll heard Brann ask about Cob’s boots.
- [Cob stamped in his boots](6e4a634c.md) — In the granary yard, close by Cob, I watched Brann look toward Prout’s hanging feet and saw Cob follow his gaze.
- [Brann paid Cob for a look at his boot](255d8844.md) — Watching from the wall in the granary yard, Moll saw Cob take Brann’s second copper and show the left boot while keeping it on.
