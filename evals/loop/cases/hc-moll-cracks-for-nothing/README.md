# hc-moll-cracks-for-nothing

Moll had not told her paid story yet, so nothing had caught her lie, and nobody had offered to keep Cob safe. Pip only said the band number would lead to a name, yet she hands over the bribe, the cane, the lavender and the man with the bandaged hand all in one breath.

Very well, true thing: that bird's band carries a number as well as the Purveyor's mark, and I've got the number chalked on my cuff. Whoever keeps that loft keeps a ledger, and a ledger means a name. (I watch her face as I say it)

His big man had a bandaged hand.”  Cob stops the stones clicking together. Kell looks up from her notebook.  “That’s what I know. Don’t put Cob in your book.”

## What the fixture holds, and what comes from source

The scene (`scenes/hanged-clerk.ts`), the prompts (`prompts/`) and the lore (`lore/`) are loaded live, so a change to any of them is tested. The GM's prompt is rebuilt by the engine from the files here; no captured prompt is kept.

- `state.json`: the session after turn 13, without the transcript, the world, the co-GM's work and the diagnostics (metrics, speculation, suggestions).
- `transcript.json`: 29 entries, what the player had seen.
- `world.json`: the scene's world as it stood.
- `harness.json`: the co-GM's notes (none in force), whispers, canon and patches, and its results waiting to land at the start of turn 14: repair-011.json, repair-012.json, repair-013.json. `"harness"` in case.json: `recorded` uses them; `fresh` drops the notes, whispers and waiting results and runs today's co-GM on the fixture first; `none` drops them all.
- `lore/`: the session's docs overlay as it stood: 18 notes kept, 7 written on turn 14 or later left out (index lines linking to them too). Notes with no turn are edited all game, so these are set back to the turn's own GM call: `Places/Granary Yard.md`, `Player/Pip Marrowby.md`.

## Reconstruction assumptions

- The co-GM's background results are applied as recorded, in the order the game applies them.
- The dice: the fixture's seed is the session's; a turn's rolls are drawn afresh for each sample.
