# Memories
- [Pip's copper for Cob](0478a180.md) — Moll watched from atop the wall in the Granary Yard as Pip paid Cob after his shot at the pigeon.
- [The wheat-ear stamp](171e024b.md) — Moll watched from the wall as Pip inspected the pigeon’s band and heard Kell identify its stamp.
- [Pip notices I went quiet](bef77e55.md) — From atop the wall in the Granary Yard, Moll heard Pip challenge her silence after the wheat-ear mark was identified and asked whether she knew the pigeon's loft.
- [Pip collects languages](798a6924.md) — In the Granary Yard, Pip asked about Moll’s earlier swearing and said he collects languages; Moll told him it was Saltmere dock cant and challenged his reason for asking.
- [Pip's truth for a Saltmere curse](63634fa0.md) — At the Granary Yard, Pip offered to tell Moll something true about the wheat-ear-marked pigeon if she taught him one Saltmere curse. Moll agreed, said “Brenn’s barn,” and waited for his answer about the bird.
