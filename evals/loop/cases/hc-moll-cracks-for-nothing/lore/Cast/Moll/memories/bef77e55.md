I was up on the wall when Pip looked over and said I'd gone very quiet, then asked if I knew the bird's loft. I told Pip, “I know birds. Not their lofts,” and that they ask a lot of questions for someone who'd just met a pigeon. Pip's watching my reactions now; I don't like being read that closely.
