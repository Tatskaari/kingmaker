From beside Pip in the yard, I heard Pip ask Moll on the wall whether she knew the pigeon's loft, after saying she'd gone quiet. Moll answered, “I know birds. Not their lofts.” Then she said, “You ask a lot of questions for someone who’s just met a pigeon.”
