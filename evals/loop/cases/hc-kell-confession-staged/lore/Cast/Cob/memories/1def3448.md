Silk-ears flipped a silver crown by my boot. He called it half now, for good faith, and said I’d get the other crown when the paper was in his hand; he said I could keep the boots. I snatched the coin. I told him he’d offered a crown for the boots and crackly bit, that I was keeping my boots, and that the other half was a crown more for a look—no picking, pulling, or taking. I took the coin, but I haven’t handed over either boot or paper, and he hasn’t got the paper in his hand. Moll said we were here at first light, then asked if he meant who got there first or who did the stringing.
