Aurelian asked the girl what she saw when she arrived, if the loft door was shut, and what her name was. She didn’t answer those questions; she said, “I saw him jump at midnight, didn’t I? Wailing about his debts. Moonlight on the whole tragic business.” I said, “That’s what she said. Cob heard it.” I was looking at Aurelian when I said it.
