Aurelian dropped the lecturing voice and said someone had cracked Prout’s skull and strangled him, and that the killer might come looking for whatever was hidden in my boot. He offered me the second crown for a look, boot stays on, and said he’d rather I read it to him than have the killer come asking. I took the crown. I kept the boot on and didn’t hand over or show him any paper; I told him, “There. You’ve had your look.”
