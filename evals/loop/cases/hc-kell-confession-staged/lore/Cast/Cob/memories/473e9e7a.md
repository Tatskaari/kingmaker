Silk-ears looked at Prout’s stockinged feet and said a man wouldn’t climb up barefoot and kick his boots to the gutter. Then he told me there was paper in my left boot and offered one silver crown for the boots and whatever was inside. I said my boots were worth two each, before the crackly bit, and told him to pay first; the boot stays on. He hadn’t paid and I didn’t give him the boot.
