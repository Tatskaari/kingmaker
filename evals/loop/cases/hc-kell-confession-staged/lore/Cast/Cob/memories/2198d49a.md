That silk-ears wizard raised his hand at Cob and said, “You, with the boots: one more stone at that body and I shall make your boots dance off without you.” Cob’s next stone hit the cobbles short of Mister Prout. Cob said, “Cob’s boots dance when Cob says they do,” and stamped; the left boot crackled. Moll told silk-ears to threaten me again and see how far he got. Sergeant Kell said the porters found Mister Prout at first light, the palace had reported him missing last night, and nobody had touched the rope because they couldn’t reach it. I’m keeping my boots and watching that posh threat-maker.
