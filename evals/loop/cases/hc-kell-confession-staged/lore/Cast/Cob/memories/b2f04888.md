Silk-ears clapped and said Moll’s midnight story didn’t fit, then had Kell come look at Mister Prout’s neck. I was by the body with my stones in my fists. Kell said there was a second cord mark, low and level, swelling on the back of his head, and no rope burn on his hands. She said the noose didn’t kill him and called it murder, but not yet a name.
