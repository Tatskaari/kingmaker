Aurelian asked what I saw when I arrived, whether the loft door was shut, and what my name was. I gave him no name or answer about the door. I answered with a sharp, teasing claim: “I saw him jump at midnight, didn’t I? Wailing about his debts. Moonlight on the whole tragic business.” Cob chimed in too quickly: “That’s what she said. Cob heard it.” I saw him looking at Aurelian as he said it.
