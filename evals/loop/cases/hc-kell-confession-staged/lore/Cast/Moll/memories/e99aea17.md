From the wall, I heard the wizard call Cob “the boots” and threaten to make them dance off without him if he threw one more stone at Prout. Cob threw it anyway; it struck the cobbles short. Cob stamped, and his left boot crackled. I told silk-ears, “Threaten him again, silk-ears, and see how far you get.” Kell said the porters found Prout at first light, the palace reported him missing last night, and no one touched the rope because they couldn’t reach it. That wizard threatened Cob in front of me; I’m not letting that pass.
