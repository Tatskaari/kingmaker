From the wall, I heard silk-ears say my story about Prout jumping at midnight didn’t add up, then call Kell over to look at his throat. Kell read out what she saw: a second cord mark low and level, a swollen back of the skull, and no rope burn on his hands. She said the noose hadn’t killed him and called it murder, not yet a name.
