From the wall, I heard Aurelian tell the sergeant Prout was in stockings, then tell Cob there was paper in the left boot and offer a silver crown for the boots and whatever was inside. Cob’s shoulders tightened at that, then he swaggered and asked two crowns for each boot, extra for the crackly bit; he said to pay first and the boot stays on. I saw the reaction and heard the claim, but I don’t know what’s actually in the boot.
