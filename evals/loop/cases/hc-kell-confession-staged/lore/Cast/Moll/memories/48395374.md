From my place on the wall, I saw Aurelian flip a silver crown to Cob, saying it was half now as good faith and offering the other crown when he had the paper; he said Cob could keep the boots. Cob took the coin, but said the boots were staying with him and asked for another crown for a look, with no picking, pulling, or taking. No boots or paper changed hands, and I didn’t hear Cob agree to let Aurelian take anything. When Aurelian asked if we’d been there when Prout was strung up or were only first to plunder, I said, “We were here at first light, weren’t we? You asking who got there first, or who did the stringing?” That was what I said; it doesn’t establish who did the hanging.
