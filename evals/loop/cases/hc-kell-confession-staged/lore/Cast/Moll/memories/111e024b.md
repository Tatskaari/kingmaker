Aurelian dropped his lecturing tone and told us someone had cracked Prout’s skull and strangled him, then staged the hanging. He said the killer might want back whatever was hidden in Cob’s boot. He offered Cob the second crown for a look, with the boot staying on and Cob reading it to him; Cob took the coin but kept the boot on, and I saw no paper passed over. Cob said, “There. You’ve had your look.”
