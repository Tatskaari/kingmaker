# Memories
- [Aurelian threatened Cob at Prout’s body](32856ee5.md) — Behind the King’s Granary, while Aurelian questioned me about Prout’s discovery and the rope, I saw him threaten Cob and heard Moll answer him.
- [Aurelian’s theory about the boots](18c6c96a.md) — At Prout’s hanging, Aurelian pointed out the body’s stockinged feet and claimed there was paper in Cob’s crackling left boot; Cob refused to hand it over without payment.
- [Aurelian pays Cob part of the boot bargain](37eb0dfe.md) — At Prout’s body in the Granary Yard, I watched Aurelian offer Cob money and ask Moll whether she and Cob were there at first light.
- [Moll’s midnight claim about Prout](adf5a30a.md) — In the Granary Yard, while Aurelian questioned the girl on the wall about what she saw at first light, I heard her answer and Cob chime in.
- [Prout was murdered, not hanged alive](886792a5.md) — At the Granary Yard, Aurelian examined Prout’s throat while I came close to look; I wrote down and read back the physical signs.
- [Aurelian paid Cob the second crown](e4e3b5b7.md) — At Prout’s body in the Granary Yard, I stood close enough to hear Aurelian address the children and watch him pay Cob; I noted the terms and what was not handed over.
