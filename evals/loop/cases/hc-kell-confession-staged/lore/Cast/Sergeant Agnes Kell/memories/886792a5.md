At Prout’s body, Aurelian pointed me to the throat. I saw the second cord mark, low and level beneath the noose burn; the back of Prout’s skull was swollen, and his palms had no rope burn. I wrote and read back: “Second cord mark, low and level. Swelling at the back of the skull. No rope burn on the hands.” The noose didn’t kill him: he was struck, strangled, then hoisted. I said it was murder, not yet a name.
