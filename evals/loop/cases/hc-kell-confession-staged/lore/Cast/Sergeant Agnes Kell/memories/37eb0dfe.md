I saw Aurelian flip a silver crown to Cob, calling it half now as good faith and offering the other crown when the paper was in his hand; he said Cob could keep the boots. Cob picked up the coin, but said Aurelian had offered a crown for the boots and crackly bit, declared he was keeping the boots, and demanded another crown for a look, with no picking, pulling, or taking. No paper or boots changed hands, and Cob did not agree to give them up. Moll said, “We were here at first light, weren’t we?” I heard the claim as her answer to Aurelian’s question, not proof of who strung Prout.
