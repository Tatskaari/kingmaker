I saw Prout’s feet were in stockings, not boots, and noted that. Aurelian said a man wouldn’t climb barefoot and kick his boots away, then told Cob there was paper in the left boot and offered one silver crown for the boots and whatever was inside. Cob demanded two crowns for each boot, extra for the crackling bit, and said to pay first—the boot stays on. I have Aurelian’s deduction, not proof of how Prout died or what is in that boot.
