Aurelian asked the girl on the wall what she saw when she arrived, whether the loft door was shut, and her name. She gave no name or answer about the door. She said, “I saw him jump at midnight, didn’t I? Wailing about his debts. Moonlight on the whole tragic business.” It sounded like a sharp, embellished answer, not a plain account. Cob quickly added, “That’s what she said. Cob heard it.”
