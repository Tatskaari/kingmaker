I heard Aurelian tell the children that someone had cracked Prout’s skull and strangled him, and that whoever staged it might come looking for what was hidden in Cob’s boot. He offered Cob the second crown for a look, with the boot to stay on and Cob to read the paper rather than hand it over. Cob took the coin but kept the boot on; I saw no paper produced or passed to Aurelian. Cob said, “There. You’ve had your look.”
