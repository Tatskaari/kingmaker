Aurelian Thorne raised a hand toward Cob and said, “You, with the boots: one more stone at that body and I shall make your boots dance off without you.” Cob threw another stone; it struck the cobbles short of Prout. Moll, from the wall, said, “Threaten him again, silk-ears, and see how far you get.” I asked whether the boots were the deceased’s. The threat was in front of me; I’ll remember it as said.
