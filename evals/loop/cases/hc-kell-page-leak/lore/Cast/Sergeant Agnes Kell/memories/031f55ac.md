In the yard, I heard Xarax offer Cob a shilling to look at his boot. Cob pocketed the coin, stamped his left boot so it crackled, and refused to let anyone inspect or take the boots. He said, “They’re Mister Prout’s, all right. Cob found ’em. Nobody’s having them. Not the watch, not my da, not some bleedin’ pawnshop.” I entered that the shilling was accepted but access refused.
