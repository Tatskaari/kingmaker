When Xarax asked about Silvertop, I said I knew the name J. Sallow from Royal Purveyor delivery notices, but had never met him and could not say whether that was an alias. Cob said Silvertop was only his name for the man, described his silver cane and flower stink, and said he did not know the man’s proper name.
