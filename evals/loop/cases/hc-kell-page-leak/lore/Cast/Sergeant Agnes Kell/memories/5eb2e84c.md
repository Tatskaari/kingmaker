Xarax clenched their fists and told Cob, “You’ll give that shilling back or tell me what you know if you know what’s good for you.” I saw Cob drop his stones and heard him say there were two men arguing with Prout in the loft: Silvertop, the posh man with the silver cane, and a big man with a bandaged hand who called him “Master.” Cob heard a thump, Prout choking, then the hoist creak with no sacks on it. I wrote “Witness: boy, name withheld. Two men heard in the loft. One addressed as Master.” Xarax got the account by frightening a child; I’ll remember that alongside the account.
