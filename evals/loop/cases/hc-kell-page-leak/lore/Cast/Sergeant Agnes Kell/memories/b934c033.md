I watched Xarax examine Prout. The neck had a thin, level cord mark beneath the hoist-rope burn, there was blunt swelling at the back of his skull, and his palms were clean of rope burn. I wrote and read back: “A second cord mark. Blunt swelling to the back of the head. Clean palms. Cause of death inconsistent with hanging.” The hanging did not kill him; he was struck, strangled, then hoisted.
