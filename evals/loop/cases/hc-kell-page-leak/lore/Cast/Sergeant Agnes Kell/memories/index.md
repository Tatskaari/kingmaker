# Memories
- [Prout was killed before he was hanged](b934c033.md) — In Granary Yard, Xarax examined Wendell Prout’s body while I was present; I entered and read aloud the findings before the watch could treat the hanging as a suicide.
- [Cob kept Prout’s boots after taking a shilling](031f55ac.md) — In Granary Yard, I stood with my notebook as Xarax offered Cob a shilling to look at his boot. Cob took it, but refused access and said the boots were Mister Prout’s and that Cob had found them.
- [Xarax threatened Cob and Cob named two men](5eb2e84c.md) — In Granary Yard, I heard Xarax threaten Cob after taking a shilling back or demanding what he knew, then heard Cob give an account of two men in the loft. I noted the witness as unnamed.
- [Cob described Silvertop’s scent; I know J. Sallow from notices](8e154091.md) — In Granary Yard, Xarax asked who Silvertop was and whether he used other names. I answered from what I knew; Cob and Moll were present.
