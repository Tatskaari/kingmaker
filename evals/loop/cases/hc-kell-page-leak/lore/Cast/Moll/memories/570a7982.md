From the wall, I saw Xarax clench their fists and threaten Cob: “You’ll give that shilling back or tell me what you know if you know what’s good for you.” Cob dropped his stones and told them there were two men with Mister Prout in the loft: Silvertop with the silver cane, and a big man with a bandaged hand who called Silvertop “Master.” Cob heard a thump, Prout choking, then the hoist creak with no sacks on it. Kell wrote “Witness: boy, name withheld.” I flung my silver crown into the mud and called Xarax a red-eyed bastard; their threat got Cob to tell where he’d been, and now I’m angry and scared for him.
