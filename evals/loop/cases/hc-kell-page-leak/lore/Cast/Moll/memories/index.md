# Memories
- [Kell said Prout’s hanging did not kill him](cab3baa7.md) — I was on the yard wall when Xarax examined Prout and Sergeant Kell read her entry aloud.
- [Cob took Xarax’s shilling but kept the boots](1b171e11.md) — I was on the Granary Yard wall, watching the gate, when Xarax offered Cob a shilling to look at his boot.
- [Cob told the loft account after Xarax threatened him](570a7982.md) — From the wall in Granary Yard, I saw Xarax threaten Cob and heard Cob tell Sergeant Kell and Xarax what he had heard in the loft. I threw my crown into the mud when he gave away that he had been there.
- [Kell named J. Sallow from Royal Purveyor notices](240db398.md) — In Granary Yard, I watched from the wall while Xarax asked who Silvertop was and whether he had other names. Kell answered from her notebook; Cob answered from the yard below.
