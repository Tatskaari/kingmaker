From the yard wall, I heard Xarax offer Cob a shilling to look at his boot. Cob pocketed it, stamped his left boot so it crackled, and still wouldn’t let Xarax look or take the boots. He said they were Mister Prout’s, he’d found them, and nobody was having them—not the watch, his da, or a pawnshop. Kell wrote it down.
