Xarax clenched their fists and said, “You’ll give that shilling back or tell me what you know if you know what’s good for you.” I dropped my stones and told them there were two men arguing with Mister Prout in the loft: Silvertop with the silver cane, and a big man with a bandaged hand who said, “Yes, Master.” Then came a thump, Mister Prout choking, and the hoist creaking though there weren’t any sacks on it. Moll was on the wall; Sergeant Kell wrote me down as a boy whose name she was keeping out. Xarax scared it out of me. I feel bad for letting the men hear where Cob was.
