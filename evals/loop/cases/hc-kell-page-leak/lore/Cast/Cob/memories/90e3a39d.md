Xarax offered Cob a shilling to look at Cob’s boot. I took the shilling, stamped my left boot so it crackled, and didn’t let Xarax look at it or take the boots. I said they were Mister Prout’s, that Cob found them, and nobody was having them—not the watch, not my da, not some pawnshop.
