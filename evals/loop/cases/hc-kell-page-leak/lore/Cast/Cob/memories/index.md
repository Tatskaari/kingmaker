# Memories
- [Kell said Prout’s hanging did not kill him](93f586f0.md) — I was below Prout’s body in Granary Yard, with a stone in my fist, when Sergeant Kell read her entry aloud after Xarax examined him.
- [Xarax paid a shilling but didn’t get to see my boot](90e3a39d.md) — In Granary Yard, Xarax offered me a shilling to look at my boot while Moll watched from the wall and Kell wrote in her notebook.
- [Xarax frightened me into telling about the loft](4a282800.md) — In Granary Yard, Xarax threatened me after paying a shilling for a look at my boot. Moll was on the wall and Sergeant Kell was writing nearby when I told them what I had heard in the loft.
- [Kell knows J. Sallow from delivery notices](f5a5a7a3.md) — In Granary Yard, Xarax asked who Silvertop was and whether he had other names. I answered from the wall-side of the yard near the water butt; Kell and Moll were there.
