Corvin counted out two silver for Cob to take off his left boot for a look and put it straight back on, and asked me to witness that the boots stayed Cob’s. I said, “The boots stay his. I witness that.” Cob said it was two silver for a look, not for taking, and that the boots stayed on Cob; he pocketed the coins without removing either boot.
