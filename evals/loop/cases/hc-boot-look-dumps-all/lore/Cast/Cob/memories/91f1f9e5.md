Corvin crouched down and said there was a silver in it if I told him something the porters didn’t see. I told him I found the boots, they were mine, and I slept where I liked; then I said the porters found Mister Prout when it got light. That wasn’t something they didn’t see, and Corvin didn’t hand over the silver.
