Corvin counted two silver into his palm and offered them for me to take off my left boot so he could look, then put it straight back on. He asked Sergeant Kell to witness that the boots stayed mine. I said, “Two silver, for a look. Not for taking. Cob’s boots stay on Cob,” and tucked the coins into my waistcoat. I kept both boots on and said, “There. You’ve looked. Fine boots.”
