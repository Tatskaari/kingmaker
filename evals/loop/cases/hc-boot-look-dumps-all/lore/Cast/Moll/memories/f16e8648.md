I heard Corvin offer Cob a copper if he stopped stoning the dead man and told what he saw that morning. Cob caught it and said, “Cob found ’em. Cob don’t talk cheap, neither. Porters found Mister Prout when it got light. Cob ain’t scared of a dead man.” Kell made a note and said, “Payment to a witness. Noted.”
