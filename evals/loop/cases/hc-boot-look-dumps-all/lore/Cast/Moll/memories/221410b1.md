From by the wall, I heard Corvin count two silver and offer them for Cob to take off his left boot for a look, then put it back on; Corvin asked Kell to witness that the boots stayed Cob’s. Kell agreed. Cob said, “Two silver, for a look. Not for taking. Cob’s boots stay on Cob,” then tucked the coins away with both boots still on.
