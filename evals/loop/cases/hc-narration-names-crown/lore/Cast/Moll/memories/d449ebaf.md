I saw Nell crouch by Cob, tap his left boot, then slide two fingers inside the cuff. Cob caught her fingers and snatched the boot to his chest, shouting, “Oi! Keep your grubby mitts off!” I dropped off the wall and got between them. Cob backed toward the water butt and warned Nell, “Touch him again and see what happens.” Nell was trying to take something from Cob; I'll watch her hands around him.
