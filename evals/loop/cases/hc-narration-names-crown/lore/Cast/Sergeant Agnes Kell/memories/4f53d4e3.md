From across the yard, I turned when the commotion started. I saw Cob jerk away from Nell and clutch his boot to his chest; Moll dropped from the wall between them. I heard Cob shout, “Oi! Keep your grubby mitts off!” then back toward the water butt and warn, “Touch him again and see what happens.” I saw Nell crouched at his boot. I can't swear to what was inside it or what Nell meant to do.
