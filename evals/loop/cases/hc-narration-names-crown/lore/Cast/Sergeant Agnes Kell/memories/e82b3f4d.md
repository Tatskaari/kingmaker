From the gate, I heard Nell ask Cob where he got the oversized black boots. Cob answered, “Found ’em,” then, “Mister Prout didn’t need ’em. And Cob does.” He stamped a toe; the left boot crackled. He said the boots were his and warned Nell not to get ideas.
