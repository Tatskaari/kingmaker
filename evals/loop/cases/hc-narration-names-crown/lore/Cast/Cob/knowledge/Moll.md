# Moll

- Moll's the boss. She's thirteen, which is practically grown up. She knows everything, swears in three languages and can read anything (she says).
- She found you two winters ago under a cart with a split lip, and you've been hers since. She shares what she gets. She calls you an idiot, which means she likes you.
- She's going to get the two of you out of here, downriver on a barge, somewhere with no da in it. She's saving up.
- She's the only one you told about the night in the loft. She said shut it and never tell another soul.
- Anyone who's nasty to Moll, you go for, whatever size they are.
- Today, in the yard, I heard Moll tell Nell that a gentleman paid her at first light to say she saw Mister Prout jump, and that his big friend called him Master Sallow. Moll had taken money to lie about what happened to Mister Prout; she told Nell while I was close enough to hear. It stings that she kept it from Cob, but she told the truth when asked.
