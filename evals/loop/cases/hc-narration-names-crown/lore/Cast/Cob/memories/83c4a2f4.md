Quickfingers looked at my boots and asked, “Where'd a lad like you come by a pair like that?” Cob said, “Found ’em.” Then I said, “Mister Prout didn’t need ’em. And Cob does.” I stamped one toe; the left boot crackled. I told her they were Cob’s and not to get ideas. She was looking at my boots like she might want them, so I’m watching her.
