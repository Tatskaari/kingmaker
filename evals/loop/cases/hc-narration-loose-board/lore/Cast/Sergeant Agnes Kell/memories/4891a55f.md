I saw Puddle press two silver crowns into Cob's hand and heard him say it was his entire fortune, save for some shillings and pennies, then ask where Cob found the boots. Cob took the crowns and said, “In the yard, obviously.” Cob then said it would cost three crowns to look and that the boots stayed on. I made a notebook entry: two crowns paid; answer, “in the yard.”
