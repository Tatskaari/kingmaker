I saw Sir Bartholomew Puddle saw through the rope above Prout and fail to catch him; Prout hit the cobbles beside Cob. Cob recoiled and his stones scattered, and Moll came down from the wall with a sharp cry. I checked Prout's neck, the back of his head, and his hands, then told Puddle: “I can sign this as murder.”
