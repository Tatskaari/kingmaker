Puddle asked Cob to show the crowns he had paid him and to say whether the gentleman with the lavender scent gave him the boots. Cob closed his fist, turned his back, edged toward the water butt, and said, “Cob told you. Found ’em. Quit asking.” I entered that he declined to show the coins or answer further; the boots remain disputed. His refusal doesn't establish who gave him the boots.
