Puddle asked Moll whether she was a liar or a wizard. She said, “I lied. I didn’t see him jump. A gentleman paid me to say it,” and added that he had a silver-topped cane. I told her she would have to say it again when it was entered; she answered, “Not to your book,” then moved close to Cob, between him and the watch. I know her claim about seeing Prout jump was false by her own admission; I do not know who paid her.
