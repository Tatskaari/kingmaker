Puddle marched to the granary’s back doors, saying he’d go up and reenact Prout’s leap. The doors were padlocked, and I told him, “Locked. The warden has the key.” Moll laughed from the wall and said, “He’ll leap up there next, shall he? Saves the trouble of opening a door.” I noted that she had claimed Prout jumped from the loft door, and said that door was barred on the inside.
