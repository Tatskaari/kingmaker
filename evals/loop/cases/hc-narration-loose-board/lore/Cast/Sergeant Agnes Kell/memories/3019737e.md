Moll told Puddle she hadn't got a good look at the gentleman's face, but described a good coat, a silver-topped cane, and enough lavender water to drown in. She opened her fist and showed one silver crown stamped with a little wheat-ear. I wrote down the description and payment, and told her I would record the payment but not her name.
