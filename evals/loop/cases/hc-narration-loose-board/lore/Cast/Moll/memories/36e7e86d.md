I heard Puddle ask Cob to show the crowns Puddle had paid him and tell whether the gentleman who paid me gave him Prout's boots. Cob closed his fist, turned his back, edged toward the water butt, and said, “Cob told you. Found ’em. Quit asking.” I stepped closer between Cob and the watch. He refused to show the money or answer the question; I don't know from this whether the gentleman gave him the boots.
