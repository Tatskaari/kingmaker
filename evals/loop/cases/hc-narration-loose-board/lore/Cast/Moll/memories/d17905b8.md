Puddle asked if I was a liar or a wizard because I said Prout jumped from a door barred on the inside. I told him I lied and hadn’t seen Prout jump; a gentleman paid me to say it, and he had a silver-topped cane. Kell said I’d have to say it again when it was entered. I refused: “Not to your book.” Then I dropped from the wall and moved close to Cob, between him and the watch. I haven’t named the gentleman.
