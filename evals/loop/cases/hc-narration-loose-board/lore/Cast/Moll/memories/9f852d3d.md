From the wall I heard Puddle call down that Prout had two neck marks—one slanting and one flat—a swelling at the back of his head, and clean palms. These were Puddle’s reported observations from up by the body, not things I examined myself. Kell said it was not a second hanging. I watched Cob go very quiet.
