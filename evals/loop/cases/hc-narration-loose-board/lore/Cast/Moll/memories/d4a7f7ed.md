Puddle asked what the gentleman looked like and whether the thing in my fist was his payment. I said I hadn't got a good look at his face: good coat, silver-topped cane, and enough lavender water to drown in. I opened my fist and showed the silver crown with the wheat-ear stamp. Kell wrote down the description and payment, and said she'd put the payment in her book but not my name.
