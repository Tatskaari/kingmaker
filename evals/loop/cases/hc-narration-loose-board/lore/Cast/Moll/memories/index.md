# Memories
- [Cob questioned about the boots](6ee4b4ae.md) — I was on the wall in Granary Yard when Puddle accused Cob of taking the dead clerk's boots.
- [Puddle's offer to Cob about the boots](bf855a1c.md) — I was on the wall in Granary Yard when Puddle knelt and spoke to Cob, and I heard Cob's reply.
- [Puddle paid Cob two crowns for the boots' location](e42a7acd.md) — From the wall in Granary Yard, I saw and heard Puddle pay Cob and Cob answer about where he found the boots.
- [Cob backed up my claim about Prout](9d6c2156.md) — In Granary Yard, I was on the wall when Puddle asked what I had seen and Cob called across the yard after my answer.
