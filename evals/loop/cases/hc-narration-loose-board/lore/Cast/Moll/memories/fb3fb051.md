I watched Puddle march to the granary’s back doors, saying he’d go up and reenact Prout’s leap. I laughed, “He’ll leap up there next, shall he? Saves the trouble of opening a door.” Kell said the back doors were locked and the warden had the key, then said the loft door was barred on the inside.
