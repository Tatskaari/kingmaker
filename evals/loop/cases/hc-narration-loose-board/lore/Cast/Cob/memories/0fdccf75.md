I heard Puddle call down that Prout had two neck marks, one slanting and one flat, a lump at the back of his head, and clean hands. That was what Puddle reported from beside the body; I couldn’t see those details from below. Kell said, “That is not a second hanging, investigator.” I’d gone quiet, and Moll was watching me from the wall.
