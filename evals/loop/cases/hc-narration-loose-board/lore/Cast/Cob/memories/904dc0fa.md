Moll said she hadn't got a good look at the gentleman's face, but he had a good coat, a silver-topped cane, and enough lavender water to drown in. She opened her fist and showed a silver crown with a little wheat-ear stamped on it. Kell wrote down the description and payment, and said she wouldn't record Moll's name.
