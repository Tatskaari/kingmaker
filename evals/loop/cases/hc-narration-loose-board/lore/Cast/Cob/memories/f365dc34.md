Puddle asked Cob to show the crowns he paid me and if the lavender gentleman gave me the boots. Cob shut his fist, turned his back, edged toward the water butt, and said, “Cob told you. Found ’em. Quit asking.” Moll stepped closer between me and the watch. Puddle's asking too much; I'm not showing him the coins, and I didn't tell him whether the gentleman gave me the boots.
