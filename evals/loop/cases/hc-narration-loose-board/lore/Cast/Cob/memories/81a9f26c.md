Puddle called Moll a liar or a wizard over her story that Prout jumped. She said she lied, hadn’t seen him jump, and a gentleman paid her to say it; she said he had a silver-topped cane. When Kell said she would have to repeat it for the book, Moll refused—“Not to your book”—and moved close to me, putting herself between me and the watch. I don’t know who that gentleman was.
