Puddle pressed two crowns into my hand and said it was his entire fortune, except for some shillings and pennies. He asked where Cob found the boots. I took the crowns, closed my fist, and said, “In the yard, obviously.” I told him it was three crowns to look, and that the boots stayed on. I got two crowns for telling him the place; he didn't pay the three crowns or look at the boots.
