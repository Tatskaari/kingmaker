I heard Corvin ask Cob where and when the boots came off Prout and say, “Tell me true and this silver's yours, and nobody's taking the boots.” Cob pushed the offered coin away with his boot, said they were his, and named a silver for looking at both and two if Corvin wanted him to take one off. Cob stamped; the left boot crackled. No silver changed hands and Cob gave no account of where or when he got the boots.
