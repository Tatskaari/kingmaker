Corvin offered Cob one silver for the folded page from inside the boot. Cob took the silver and handed Corvin the page; the boot stayed with Cob. Corvin said it was a dead man's letter and ought to reach the King. When he asked me quietly, I told him J. Sallow was an under-purveyor for grain, that I knew the signature from Assembly delivery notices but had never seen Sallow, and that the Royal Purveyor's office was in the palace back courts.
