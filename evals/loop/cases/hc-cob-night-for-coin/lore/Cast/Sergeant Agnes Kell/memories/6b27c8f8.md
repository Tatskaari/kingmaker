I heard Corvin call Moll's language hedge-folk talk, guess she spoke goblin, and ask what she wanted for who was in the yard last night. Moll answered, “Goblin? That’s a new one. You want to buy a story, ask the boy. I’ve already sold mine.” Cob looked sharply up at Sallow's name and stopped the stones. I said the page ties wagons to Sallow's signature, but it does not put him in the yard last night or tell how Prout died.
