# Memories
- [Corvin paid Cob for his account](aaac74f1.md) — At the Granary Yard, I heard Corvin offer Cob a copper for stopping the stones and telling what he saw, then saw Cob take it and speak; Moll was there too.
- [Corvin's conditional silver offer to Cob](3e5e0da2.md) — I was present in the Granary Yard and heard Corvin's low-voiced exchange with Cob; I noted the offer in my notebook. No silver changed hands in this exchange.
- [Corvin says he is at the King's pleasure](412d9de6.md) — At the Granary Yard, Corvin introduced himself to me as “at the King's pleasure” while asking to buy my reserved judgement; Cob and Moll were there too.
- [Corvin's offer about Prout's boots](091c323f.md) — At the Granary Yard, I heard Corvin offer Cob silver for an account of where and when Prout's boots came off him, with a pledge that no one would take the boots; Cob refused the coin.
- [Corvin paid Cob two silver for a look at his boots](234d908f.md) — In the Granary Yard, I witnessed Corvin's offer and Cob's acceptance, including their terms about the left boot and its ownership.
- [Prout's hidden grain ledger and warning](457d3e92.md) — At the Granary Yard, after Cob removed his left boot for Corvin's paid look, a page sewn into its lining exposed records about grain wagons and Prout's warning to Kell.
- [Corvin bought Cob's page](52eac932.md) — In the Granary Yard, I witnessed Corvin pay Cob one silver for the folded page from his boot and watched Cob hand it over while keeping the boot. I also answered Corvin's question about Sallow and the Purveyor's office.
- [Corvin asks Moll who was in the yard](6b27c8f8.md) — At the Granary Yard, I was beside the investigation and heard Corvin ask Moll about her language and offer to buy an account of who had been in the yard last night; I also heard Moll's refusal and recorded the limits of what the page showed.
