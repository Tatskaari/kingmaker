Corvin flipped a copper to Cob, offering it if Cob stopped stoning Prout and told what he saw that morning. Cob caught it and said, “Cob found ’em. Cob don’t talk cheap, neither. Porters found Mister Prout when it got light. Cob ain’t scared of a dead man.” I noted: “Payment to a witness.”
