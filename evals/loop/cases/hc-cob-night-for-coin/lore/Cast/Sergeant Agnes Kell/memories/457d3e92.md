I watched Cob take off his left boot for Corvin's look, and a folded page sewn into its lining came out. I read entries for wagons marked “lost to goblins,” countersigned “J. Sallow, for the Royal Purveyor,” with Prout's notes that the wagons weighed the same at the river warehouses two days later; at the bottom he wrote, “Not goblins. Who pays the hedge-folk?” Corvin was there. I told him, “He came to me three days ago. Asked how to report false entries in the King’s rolls. I told him to take it to the palace.” Seeing the page, I said, “I should have listened.”
