I took off my left boot for Corvin to look, like I agreed, and a folded page was sewn inside its lining. I took the boot straight back and started pulling it on. I heard Sergeant Kell say Prout had come to her three days earlier asking how to report false entries in the King's rolls, and that she had told him to take it to the palace. I don't know what was written on the page.
