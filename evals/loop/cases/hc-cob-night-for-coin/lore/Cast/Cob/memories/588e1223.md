Corvin held out a silver and asked where and when the boots came off Mister Prout. He said, “Tell me true and this silver's yours, and nobody's taking the boots.” I shoved the coin away with my boot. I said a silver was for looking at both boots, two if he wanted me to take one off, and that they were mine. I stamped and the left boot crackled. I didn't tell him where or when I got them, and he didn't pay me.
