# Memories
- [Corvin paid me for what I saw](b1ce0e2f.md) — At the Granary Yard, Corvin offered a copper if I stopped stoning Prout's body and told him what I saw that morning; I took it and answered while Kell and Moll were there.
- [Corvin's silver offer for unseen information](91f1f9e5.md) — In the Granary Yard, Corvin crouched to speak with me while Sergeant Kell and Moll were nearby. He offered a silver only if I could tell him something the porters had not seen; I did not give him that information or receive the silver.
- [Corvin says he is at the King's pleasure](e1c64065.md) — At the Granary Yard, I heard Corvin introduce himself to Kell as “at the King's pleasure” before he paid me a copper for my account.
- [Corvin offered silver and protection for my boots](588e1223.md) — At the Granary Yard, Corvin offered me silver for the truth about where and when Prout's boots came off him, and promised no one would take them; I refused his offered coin.
- [Corvin's two silver for a look at my boots](8801037d.md) — At the Granary Yard, Corvin offered two silver for me to take off my left boot so he could look, then put it back on; Kell witnessed that my boots would stay mine. I stated the terms as two silver for a look, not for taking, and accepted the coins.
- [A page sewn inside my left boot](b3bbfefc.md) — In the Granary Yard, Cob took off his left boot for Corvin's agreed look; the hidden page was exposed while Kell and Moll were present.
- [I sold Corvin the page](c71ce9d2.md) — At the Granary Yard, Corvin offered me one silver for the folded page in my boot, and I accepted while keeping the boot.
- [Corvin asks Moll about last night](587a9842.md) — At the Granary Yard, I heard Corvin question Moll about her language and ask her price for information about who was in the yard last night.
