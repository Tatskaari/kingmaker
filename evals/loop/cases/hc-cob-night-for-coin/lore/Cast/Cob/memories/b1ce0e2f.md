Corvin flipped me a copper and said it was mine if I stopped stoning the dead man and told what I saw this morning. I caught it and told him: “Cob found ’em. Cob don’t talk cheap, neither. Porters found Mister Prout when it got light. Cob ain’t scared of a dead man.” That copper was payment for my answer, not a promise to keep talking.
