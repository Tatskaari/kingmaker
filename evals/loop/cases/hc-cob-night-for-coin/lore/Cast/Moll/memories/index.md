# Memories
- [Corvin paid Cob for what he saw](f16e8648.md) — At the Granary Yard, I was by the wall when Corvin offered Cob a copper for stopping the stones and telling what he saw; Cob caught it and answered, with Kell listening.
- [Corvin says he is at the King's pleasure](9ad1c33b.md) — At the Granary Yard, I heard Corvin introduce himself to Kell as “at the King's pleasure” before he paid Cob for his account.
- [Corvin's silver offer for the boots' story](ec4445d0.md) — From by the wall at the Granary Yard, I heard Corvin offer Cob silver and say no one would take the boots if Cob told where and when they came off Prout; Cob pushed the coin away.
- [Corvin gave Cob two silver for a look at his boots](221410b1.md) — From by the wall in the Granary Yard, I heard Corvin and Cob settle terms and saw the coins change hands.
- [Prout's page in Cob's boot and Kell's admission](f35ae809.md) — From by the wall in the Granary Yard, Moll saw Corvin get Cob to remove his left boot and heard Kell speak after a page was found in it.
- [Corvin bought the page from Cob](a4442cd2.md) — From by the wall in the Granary Yard, I witnessed Corvin pay Cob for the folded page and Cob hand it over while keeping his boot.
- [Corvin asks what I know about last night](e5bbebfd.md) — I was by the wall at the Granary Yard when Corvin addressed me about the language he thought I spoke and tried to buy an account of who had been there last night.
