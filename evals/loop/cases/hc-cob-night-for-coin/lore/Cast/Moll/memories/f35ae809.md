From by the wall, I saw Cob take off his left boot for Corvin and a folded page come out of its lining. I heard Sergeant Kell say, “He came to me three days ago. Asked how to report false entries in the King’s rolls. I told him to take it to the palace.” She looked at the page and said, “I should have listened.” I couldn't read the page from where I stood.
