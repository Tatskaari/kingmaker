From by the wall, I heard Corvin guess my other language was hedge-folk talk, maybe goblin, and ask what my price was for who had been in the yard last night. I told him, “Goblin? That’s a new one. You want to buy a story, ask the boy. I’ve already sold mine.” I heard Kell say the page did not put Sallow in the yard last night or say how Prout died.
