By the wall, I heard Corvin ask Cob where and when Prout's boots came off him, offering a silver if Cob told the truth and saying nobody would take the boots. Cob shoved the coin away with a boot, said the boots were his, and said a silver was for looking at both and two if Corvin wanted him to take one off. Cob stamped, and I heard the left boot crackle. He did not give Corvin the account, and no silver changed hands.
