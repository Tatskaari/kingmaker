# Memories
- [Moll’s claim about midnight](e4b2c35e.md) — At the Granary Yard, while Maren asked the girl on the wall whether she had seen anyone around the granary, Kell heard her answer in the yard.
- [Moll admits she was paid to lie](81f257a4.md) — At the Granary Yard, after Maren pointed out that the loft door was barred from inside and asked Moll about the coin in her fist, Kell heard Moll's admission and saw the coin.
- [Moll describes Master Sallow](8fc97b08.md) — At the Granary Yard, beside Maren as she asked Moll her name and about the man who paid her, Kell heard Moll’s identification and description.
- [Cob says he found the boots](53f771d6.md) — At the Granary Yard, while Maren questioned Cob about his boots and whether he saw the gentleman this morning, Kell heard Cob answer and Moll step in from the wall.
- [Cob refuses to answer about Prout](63c30b16.md) — At the Granary Yard, after Maren copied the paper from Cob’s boot and returned it, Kell witnessed Maren ask whether Prout had given Cob the boots and when Cob last saw him alive.
- [Prout’s page in Cob’s boot](9d660b9e.md) — At the Granary Yard, after Maren asked Cob’s permission to inspect his crackling left boot, Kell looked over Maren’s shoulder at the folded page sewn into its lining.
- [Moll doesn't know if the man will return](03667723.md) — At the Granary Yard, after Maren stepped back from Cob and asked Moll whether the bandaged man was coming back, Kell heard Moll answer and added it to her notes.
- [I promised to keep Cob and Moll here](3c5c7757.md) — At the Granary Yard, Maren asked me to keep Cob and Moll near while she searched the granary exterior; I agreed and stayed with them.
- [Granary keys and yesterday’s sweep](0c57c6d5.md) — At the Granary Yard, after Maren asked who swept the yard and who held the granary keys, I explained the Assembly preparations and the access arrangements.
