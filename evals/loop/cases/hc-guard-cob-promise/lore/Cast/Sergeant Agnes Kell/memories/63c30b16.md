Maren copied the page from Cob’s left boot and handed it back carefully, saying he had kept it safe and that mattered. She asked whether Prout gave him the boots or asked him to hold them, and when Cob last saw Prout alive. Cob snatched the boot back, turned his shoulder, and said, “Cob told you, they’re Cob’s. Quit poking at it.” Moll dropped from the wall, moved between them, and said, “Come on. Now.” I saw the clerk still hanging above us.
