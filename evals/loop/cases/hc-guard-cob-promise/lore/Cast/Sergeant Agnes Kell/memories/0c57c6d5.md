I told Maren the porters swept the yard yesterday for the Assembly. The warden keeps a key and is at the palace counting provisions; her runner returned with the message “After the Assembly.” The Purveyor’s under-purveyors also have keys. The sacks come through the yard doors, and the porters’ ladders are locked inside.
