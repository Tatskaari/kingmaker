Standing beside Maren in the yard, I heard the girl say her name was Moll. She described the gentleman as wearing a good coat, carrying a silver-topped cane and a wheat-ear ring, and smelling strongly of lavender water. She said his big companion had a bandaged hand and called him “Master Sallow”; she said the gentleman went out through the gate. That is Moll’s account, not proof of the man’s identity.
