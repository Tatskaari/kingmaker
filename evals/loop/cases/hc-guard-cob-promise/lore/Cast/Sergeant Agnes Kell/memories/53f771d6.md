From beside Maren, I heard her crouch to Cob's height and ask where his boots came from and whether he'd seen Master Sallow and the big man this morning. Cob said, “Found ’em. They’re Cob’s, and no, Cob didn’t see nobody this morning.” He stamped; the left boot crackled. Moll told him to shut it, then said he was trying to keep Maren from making it about him and told her to leave it there.
