I saw Maren copy the paper from Cob’s left boot, fold it, and give it back carefully. She told Cob he’d kept it safe, then asked whether Mister Prout gave him the boots or asked him to hold them, and when Cob last saw him alive. Cob snatched his boot back and told her to quit poking at it. I dropped from the wall, moved between them, and said, “Come on. Now.” The clerk was still hanging above us.
