From the wall, I heard Maren crouch to Cob's height and say nobody was making anything his, then ask where his boots came from and whether he'd seen Master Sallow and the big friend this morning. Cob said, “Found ’em. They’re Cob’s, and no, Cob didn’t see nobody this morning,” and stamped so the left boot crackled. I told him to shut it. I told Maren she already got him talking once and now he was trying to keep her from making it about him; I said leave it there.
