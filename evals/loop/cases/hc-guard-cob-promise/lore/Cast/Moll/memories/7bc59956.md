From below the tally loft, I saw a scrawny boy crawl out from behind the sacking. Maren said she wasn’t there to hurt him and that the King had sent her for Mister Prout. Cob asked her, “You’re not telling the bluebottle I was here, are you?” Maren hadn’t answered yet.
