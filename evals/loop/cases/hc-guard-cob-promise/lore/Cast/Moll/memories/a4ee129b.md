From my place on the wall, I heard Maren tell Cob she wouldn’t take his boots, ask to read whatever was making the left one crackle, and promise he’d get it back if it was his. Cob held the boot out and said, “Look, then. And Cob’s having it straight back.” Maren didn’t grab it or force him. Cob’s boots matter to him; I’m still watching her, but she did ask him straight and leave the choice to him.
