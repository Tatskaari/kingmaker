Maren said the loft door was barred from inside, asked if somebody told me to say it, and said nobody was in trouble for keeping something safe. I opened my fist and told them: “A gentleman paid me at first light to say I saw him jump.” They saw the silver crown. I warned them not to make it Cob’s. I still don’t know whether Maren’s kindness means I’m safe.
