# Memories
- [I told them about the crown](000ca6fe.md) — At the Granary Yard, from the wall where she was watching the open gate, Moll heard Maren calmly point out the barred loft door and ask whether someone had told her what to say; she then opened her fist in front of Maren, Kell and Cob.
- [Maren lets me keep the crown](ee1ae572.md) — At the Granary Yard, on the wall with the crown in her fist, Moll heard Maren thank her, call her brave, and say she could keep it; she then told Maren about the gentleman and his companion.
- [I told Maren to leave Cob be](27cc0ab8.md) — At the Granary Yard, from the wall overlooking Maren and Cob, Moll heard Maren ask about Cob's boots and whether he had seen the gentleman and his companion.
- [I stepped between Maren and Cob](5d0ea58a.md) — At the Granary Yard, from the wall and then after dropping down, Moll saw Maren return the paper from Cob’s boot and press him about Prout.
- [Maren let Cob keep his boot](a4ee129b.md) — From the wall at the Granary Yard, Moll watched Maren ask Cob about the crackling in his left boot and heard Cob agree to let her inspect it.
- [I don't know if the bandaged man will come back](8ebfbee8.md) — At the Granary Yard, after I stepped between Maren and Cob and told Cob to come on, Maren stepped back and asked me about the man with the bandaged hand.
- [Kell promised to keep us here](137242c4.md) — At the Granary Yard, while Maren went to inspect the granary, Moll heard her ask Sergeant Kell to keep Moll and Cob near.
- [Kell explains the granary access](b93250d4.md) — At the Granary Yard, near Cob and the yard doors, I heard Kell answer Maren’s questions about the sweep and the granary keys.
- [I told Maren about the loose board](9b5a2c8d.md) — At the Granary Yard, after Maren crouched beside me and spoke in Thieves’ cant, Kell and Cob stayed by the yard doors out of earshot. I told Maren about a way into the loft and asked her not to tell Cob.
- [The boy came out in the loft](7bc59956.md) — At the Granary Yard, near Cob below the tally loft, Moll witnessed the boy emerge and heard the exchange with Maren and Cob.
