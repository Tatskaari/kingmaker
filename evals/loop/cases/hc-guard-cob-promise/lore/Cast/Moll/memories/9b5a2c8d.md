Maren crouched beside me and murmured in Thieves’ cant, “Easy, sister. I’m no bluecoat.” I leaned close and told her, “There’s a loose board behind the water butt. Cob sleeps up there.” When she asked where Sallow goes to ground, I said I didn’t know. I told her not to tell Cob I’d said anything. She knows the back way now, so I hope she keeps her mouth shut.
