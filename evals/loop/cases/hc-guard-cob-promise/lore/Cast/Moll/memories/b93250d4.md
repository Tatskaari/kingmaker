I heard Kell say the porters swept the yard yesterday for the Assembly. She said the warden keeps a key and is at the palace counting provisions, and her runner came back with “After the Assembly.” Kell said the Purveyor’s under-purveyors have keys too. The sacks come through the yard doors, and the porters’ ladders are locked inside.
