Maren thanked me, said I was brave, and told me to keep the crown. She didn’t try to take it. My fist loosened, and I gave her my name and what I knew: good coat, silver-topped cane, wheat-ear ring, stink of lavender; his big friend had a bandaged hand and called him “Master Sallow.” I said the gentleman went out through the gate. Maren feels kinder than I expected, but I still don’t know if that means Cob and I are safe.
