Maren said she wouldn’t take Cob’s boots and asked if she could read what was inside, saying I could watch and get it back if it was mine. Cob gave her the left boot and said, “Look, then. And Cob’s having it straight back.” She found a folded page sewn into the lining. Cob can’t read what’s on it, but I heard Kell say Mister Prout came to her about false entries three days before he died, she told him to take it to the palace, and “I should have listened.” Maren asked instead of grabbing my boots and said they stay mine; that’s better than a grown-up who just takes.
