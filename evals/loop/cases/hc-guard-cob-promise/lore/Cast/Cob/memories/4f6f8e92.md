I heard Kell say the porters swept the yard yesterday for the Assembly. She said the warden keeps a key and is at the palace counting provisions, and that her runner came back saying “After the Assembly.” Kell said the Purveyor’s under-purveyors have keys too. She pointed out the sacks come through the yard doors and the porters’ ladders are locked inside.
