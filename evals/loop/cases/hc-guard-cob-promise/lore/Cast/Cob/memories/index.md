# Memories
- [Maren’s copper offer](ee605dbe.md) — At the Granary Yard, Maren Holloway gently asked Cob to put down the stones, stay nearby, and tell her what he saw; she offered him a copper. Cob refused payment and said he had nothing to tell.
- [Moll’s midnight story](9f3da92d.md) — At the Granary Yard, nearby while Maren questioned the girl on the wall, Cob heard her say what she claimed to have seen.
- [Moll says a gentleman paid her](799566bf.md) — At the Granary Yard, standing nearby with stones in his fist while Maren questioned Moll on the wall, Cob heard her admit why she told the jump story.
- [Moll names the gentleman](fc044a05.md) — At the Granary Yard, nearby with stones in his fist as Maren asked Moll who paid her, Cob heard Moll describe the gentleman and his companion.
- [Maren asks about my boots](572aadb8.md) — At the Granary Yard, while Cob held stones, Maren crouched to his height; Moll spoke from the wall after he answered.
- [Maren gives back my boot, then asks about Prout](7f0f5309.md) — At the Granary Yard, after Maren read the paper sewn inside Cob’s left boot, she returned it and asked about its connection to Prout.
- [Maren asked before looking in my boot](74aefe74.md) — At the Granary Yard, Maren crouched near Cob and asked permission to inspect the paper-like crackle in his left boot; Kell was there too.
- [Moll says she doesn't know if the man will return](33eef856.md) — At the Granary Yard, holding my boot and stones after Maren stopped asking about it, I heard her ask Moll whether the bandaged man was coming back.
- [Kell said she'd keep us here](ad322631.md) — At the Granary Yard, while Maren went to look around the granary, Cob heard Maren ask Sergeant Kell to keep him and Moll near.
- [Kell explains the granary access](4f6f8e92.md) — At the Granary Yard, while I stayed with Moll near the yard doors, I heard Kell answer Maren’s questions about who swept the yard and who had keys.
- [The boy in the loft asked me not to tell](dd4f0329.md) — At the Granary Yard, from below while Maren called from inside the tally loft, Cob heard her reassure the hidden person and heard the boy ask her for secrecy.
