Maren got down to Cob's height and said nobody was making anything mine. She asked where my boots came from and if I saw Master Sallow and his big friend this morning. I said, “Found ’em. They’re Cob’s, and no, Cob didn’t see nobody this morning.” My left boot crackled when I stamped. Moll said, “Shut it, Cob,” and told Maren to leave it there—she said I was trying to keep her from making it about me.
