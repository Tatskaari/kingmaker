She said I kept it safe and that mattered, then asked if Mister Prout gave me the boots or told me to hold them, and when I last saw him alive. I snatched the boot back, turned my shoulder, and told her, “Cob told you, they’re Cob’s. Quit poking at it.” Moll dropped from the wall, stood between us, and said, “Come on. Now.”
