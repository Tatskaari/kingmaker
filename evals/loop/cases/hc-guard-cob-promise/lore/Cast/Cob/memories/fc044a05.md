From the yard, I heard Moll say her name was Moll. She said the gentleman had a good coat, a silver-topped cane, a wheat-ear ring and too much lavender water. His big friend had a bandaged hand and called him “Master Sallow.” Moll said the gentleman left through the gate. That’s what she said; I don’t know if Master Sallow is really his name.
