From below the tally loft, I heard Maren say, “I’m not here to hurt anyone,” and that the King had sent her for Mister Prout. A scrawny boy came out from behind the sacking. I asked Maren, “You’re not telling the bluebottle I was here, are you?” She hadn’t answered yet.
