# hc-coin-buys-look

One turn after Cob refused on a natural 1 and edged off to bolt, he sells a look in the boot he has fought for all scene, for coin, though a grown-up paying to look at his boots is exactly what he distrusts; Moll, who has shielded him all scene, brokers the deal.

- **Scene:** `hanged-clerk`, turn 10 (the fixture is the session after turn 9, beat `doubt`).
- **Source:** session `~/git/kingmaker-scratch/loop/r1-1017/runs/sessions/g06`, game `g06`.
- **The player said:** (I stick one big leg out across the gap so he can't slip off, then turn to the girl) You. Shawl. You're his big sister or his boss. Tell him. What's it cost? (I dig out a coin and hold it up)
- **What came back (recorded):** Your leg bars the gap. The girl in the shawl’s eyes narrow at the coin.  “What’s it cost? You’ve got it backwards, big woman. You want a look in his boot, you pay Cob.”  Cob peeks around the water butt, then stands taller in the boots. “A silver crown. And Cob keeps the boots. No grabbing, no taking ’em off, no bluebottle tricks.”  Kell’s pencil taps once against her notebook. “A payment offered for access to the boy’s boot. Noted.”  Cob holds out his left foot, keeping the boot firmly on. “Pay first.”
- **Rubric:** `character`, severity 3, on "A silver crown. And Cob keeps the boots.".
