Grunda said Prout had a slanting rope mark and a level line around his throat, and that her army experience told her someone choked him before hanging him. She told Kell this was no suicide and to write it big. Kell said she’d sign him in as a murder, but wouldn’t name anyone without evidence; she said the dead-cart comes at noon.
