I heard Grunda crouch and ask Cob to show what was inside his boot, saying, “I won’t take it from you.” Cob pulled his foot away, covered it with both hands and said, “No! Cob said they’re Cob’s. You said you wouldn’t take ’em, and now you want to poke about in ’em.” When he backed toward the water butt, I dropped from the wall, stood between them and told Grunda, “You heard him. Hands off his boots.” She didn’t inspect it.
