Grunda climbed down and sat on the water butt, then told Cob his big boots were Mister Prout's, not Da's. She said she didn't care that Cob took them because dead men don't need boots, but the crackle inside might catch the strangler, and asked if I wanted to help. I covered the left boot, backed toward the butt and said, “Cob don’t know nothing, and you’re not having them.” Moll got in front of me and told the investigator I'd answered.
