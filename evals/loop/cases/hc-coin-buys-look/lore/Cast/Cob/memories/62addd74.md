Grunda left my boots alone and went to the hanging man. She told the sergeant they should cut him down so he wouldn’t hang there for pigeons, then pulled the rope. It barely moved and the man stayed up. The sergeant said the rope was fastened inside, above the loft door, and they’d need the ladder or loft access.
