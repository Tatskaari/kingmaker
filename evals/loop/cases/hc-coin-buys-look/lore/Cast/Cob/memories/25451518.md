I saw Grunda pat down Wendell Prout’s coat and pockets from atop the water butt. She asked about shoes, a purse, or papers. His feet were in stockings, no boots. Sergeant Kell said the porters found his satchel open and empty and his spectacles by the gate, and that she’d recorded both.
