Grunda crouched and put her hand out, saying, “Something in there, little man. Show Grunda. I won’t take it from you.” Cob pulled his foot back, covered my boot with both hands, and said, “No! Cob said they’re Cob’s. You said you wouldn’t take ’em, and now you want to poke about in ’em.” I backed toward the water butt. Moll got down from the wall and stood between us, telling Grunda, “You heard him. Hands off his boots.” She didn’t get to look inside.
