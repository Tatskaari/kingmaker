Grunda said Prout had two marks on his throat—a slanting rope mark and another line going straight around—and that her army experience told her someone choked him from behind before hanging him. She told Kell it wasn’t suicide and to write it big. Kell said she’d sign him in as a murder, but wouldn’t write a name without evidence; the dead-cart comes at noon.
