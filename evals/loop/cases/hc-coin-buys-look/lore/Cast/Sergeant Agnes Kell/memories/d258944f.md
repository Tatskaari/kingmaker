While I held the water butt steady, Grunda patted down Prout’s coat and pockets and asked, “What’s he got on him? Shoes, purse, papers?” I told her the porters found his satchel open and empty, and his spectacles by the gate; I’d recorded both. I saw Prout’s feet were in stockings, with no boots on them.
