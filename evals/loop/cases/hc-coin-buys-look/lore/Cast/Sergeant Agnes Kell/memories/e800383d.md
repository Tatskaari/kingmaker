I watched Grunda crouch to Cob’s level and hold out her hand, palm up. She said, “Something in there, little man. Show Grunda. I won’t take it from you.” Cob snatched his foot back, covered the boot with both hands and said, “No! Cob said they’re Cob’s. You said you wouldn’t take ’em, and now you want to poke about in ’em.” He backed toward the water butt. Moll came down from the wall and stood between them: “You heard him. Hands off his boots.” No inspection took place.
