While I steadied the butt, I saw the slanting noose mark and the level line around Prout’s throat. Grunda said her army experience told her the level line came first and that someone strangled him before hanging him. I wrote her finding down and told her I’d sign Prout in as a murder, but wouldn’t write a name without evidence. The dead-cart comes at noon.
