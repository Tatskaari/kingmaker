From near the open gate, I heard Grunda say, “Not a bluebottle. I’m Grunda. King sent me.” Cob answered that he had found the boots and that they were his da’s. The girl on the wall disputed that, saying he’d need a second da to fill them; Cob stamped, and his left boot crackled.
