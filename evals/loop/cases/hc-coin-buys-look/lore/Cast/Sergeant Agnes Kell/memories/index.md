# Memories
- [Grunda and Cob’s boots](df49e511.md) — At the Granary Yard, Kell was watching with her notebook as Grunda introduced herself and asked Cob about his oversized boots.
- [Cob refused the boot inspection](e800383d.md) — At the Granary Yard, Kell watched Grunda crouch and ask Cob to show what was inside his boot; Cob refused and Moll stepped between them.
- [Grunda tried to lower Prout](d397d607.md) — At the Granary Yard, Kell stood near Grunda as she tried to bring Wendell Prout down from the hoist.
- [I offered to hold a ladder for Grunda](58765a18.md) — At the Granary Yard, after Grunda said they should go up to reach the hoist fastening, I offered help with a ladder if one appeared.
- [Grunda moved the water butt beneath Prout](2834bfdd.md) — At the Granary Yard, Grunda shifted the full water butt beneath Wendell Prout so she could reach the body; Kell steadied it as Grunda climbed.
- [Grunda called Prout’s death murder](7e0d522a.md) — At the Granary Yard, while I steadied the water butt beneath Prout, Grunda described the marks on his throat and asked me to record her conclusion; I answered that I would sign him in as murdered, but not name anyone without evidence.
- [Grunda searched Prout’s pockets](d258944f.md) — At the Granary Yard, while I steadied the water butt beneath Prout, Grunda searched his coat and asked what he had on him.
- [Cob refused to help Grunda](6bbd5e96.md) — At the Granary Yard, while I stood with my notebook near the water butt, I watched Grunda climb down and sit rather than loom over Cob, then ask him to help catch Prout’s killer.
