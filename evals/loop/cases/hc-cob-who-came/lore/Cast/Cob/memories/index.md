# Memories
- [Brann found Cob's castle](3e7175ea.md) — In the yard, I saw Brann pull the crooked board from the wall and show my hidey-hole. Moll came down and told him to leave it.
- [Brann spoke to me by the gap](6647e003.md) — At the crooked board’s child-sized gap in the granary yard, Brann crouched to Cob’s height while Moll watched from the wall and Kell stood by the gate.
- [Brann asked about my boots](dbd795a1.md) — At the granary yard, Brann spoke to Cob while Kell watched from the gate and Moll stood closer by.
- [Brann looked at Mister Prout’s feet](89750029.md) — In the granary yard, near the water butt, I followed Brann’s look up to Mister Prout’s hanging feet, then stamped in my boots.
- [Brann paid two coppers to look at my boot](9c344cd6.md) — In the granary yard, Cob took Brann’s second copper and showed the left boot while keeping it on.
- [I told Brann about the paper in my boot](455f3994.md) — In the granary yard by the water butt, after Brann said, “Not the boot. The paper,” Cob admitted what Mister Prout had put there.
- [Brann says my boots stay mine](3f48bbf3.md) — In the granary yard, after Brann had paid two coppers to look at my boot, I agreed to show him the paper in its lining only after he promised the boots would stay mine.
- [Brann took Mister Prout’s paper](93784dae.md) — In the granary yard by the water butt, Cob let Brann take the paper from his left boot while keeping the boot.
