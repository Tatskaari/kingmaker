Brann said, “Boots stay yours. Just the paper.” I told him, “All right. Boots stay Cob’s.” I took off the left boot but kept it clutched to my chest, and turned the opening toward him. The stiff folded paper Mister Prout put in the lining was there. I said, “That’s Mister Prout’s.” I showed Brann the paper; I didn’t give him the boot or the paper.
