I let Brann take Mister Prout’s folded paper from the lining of my left boot. Brann said the boots stayed mine and took only the paper. Sergeant Kell read out that it was a cut page from the Royal Granary Roll, listing King’s Road wagons marked “lost to goblins” and signed “J. Sallow, for the Royal Purveyor.”
