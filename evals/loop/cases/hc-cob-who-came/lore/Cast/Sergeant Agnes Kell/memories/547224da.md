Brann took a folded, cut page from the lining of Cob’s left boot; Cob kept the boot, after agreeing that only the paper would be taken. I read the page over Brann’s shoulder: it listed King’s Road wagons marked “lost to goblins,” each with the countersignature “J. Sallow, for the Royal Purveyor.” I noted that this page was recovered from Wendell Prout’s boot. That is what the page says; I have not established whether the wagons were truly lost or who was responsible.
