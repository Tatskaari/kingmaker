From beside the gate, I saw Brann look up at Prout’s stockinged feet hanging above the cobbles. Cob followed his look, dropped his eyes to his own boots, stamped once, and said, “Good boots.” His left boot gave a faint crackle. I saw the feet and heard Cob’s words; I don’t know whose boots they are.
