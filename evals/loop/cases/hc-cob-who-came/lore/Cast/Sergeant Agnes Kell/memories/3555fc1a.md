From beside the gate, I heard Brann tell Cob, “Boots stay yours. Just the paper.” Cob answered, “All right. Boots stay Cob’s,” then took off the left boot while keeping it clutched to his chest and turned its opening toward Brann. I saw a stiff, folded paper tucked into the lining. Cob said, “That’s Mister Prout’s.” He showed it, but did not hand it over.
