By the gate, pencil over my open notebook, I heard Brann crouch to Cob’s height and say, “Your castle. You saw.” Cob looked toward the boots and said, “Cob sleeps where Cob likes.” He edged toward the water butt and said no more. I can swear to those words, not to what he meant by them.
