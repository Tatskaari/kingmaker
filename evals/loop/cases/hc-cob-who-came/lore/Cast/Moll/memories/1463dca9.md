From the wall, I heard Brann say, “Boots stay yours. Just the paper.” Cob answered, “All right. Boots stay Cob’s,” then crouched and took off the left boot, holding it to his chest as he turned its opening toward Brann. I saw a stiff folded paper in the lining, and heard Cob say, “That’s Mister Prout’s.” He showed it; I did not see him hand it over.
