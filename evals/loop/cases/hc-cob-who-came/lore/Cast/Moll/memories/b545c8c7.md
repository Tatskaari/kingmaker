From the wall I heard Brann crouch to Cob’s height and say, “Your castle. You saw.” Cob looked toward the boots and answered, “Cob sleeps where Cob likes.” He edged toward the water butt and shut up. Kell was by the gate with her notebook open. I stayed quiet.
