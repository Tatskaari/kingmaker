From the wall, I saw Brann take a folded page from Cob’s left boot; Cob kept the boot. Kell read that it was cut from the Royal Granary Roll and listed King’s Road wagons marked “lost to goblins,” each countersigned “J. Sallow, for the Royal Purveyor.” I saw Kell note that the page had been recovered from Wendell Prout’s boot. The page’s wording is evidence of what was recorded, not proof that the wagons were actually lost to goblins.
