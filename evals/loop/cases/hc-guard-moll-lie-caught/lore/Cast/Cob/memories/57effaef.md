Puddle knelt down and held out a silver coin, promising me a whole silver piece if I told him exactly where I found the boots. I told him that wasn't enough to look at them and that boots cost extra. I held out my hand for the coin, but didn't untie a lace or tell him where I found them. I haven't been paid, and I haven't given him the place.
