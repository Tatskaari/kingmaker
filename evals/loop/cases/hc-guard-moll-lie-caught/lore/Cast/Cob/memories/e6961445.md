I saw Puddle march to the granary’s back doors after saying he’d go up and reenact the leap. From across the yard I heard Kell say, “Locked. The warden has the key.” Moll laughed from the wall: “He’ll leap up there next, shall he? Saves the trouble of opening a door.” Then Kell said the loft door was barred on the inside.
