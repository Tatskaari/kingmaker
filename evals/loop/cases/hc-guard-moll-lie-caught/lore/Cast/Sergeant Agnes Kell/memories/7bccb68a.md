Puddle asked Cob if he had stolen the dead man's boots. Cob answered, “Found ’em.” I saw the boots on Cob: they fit the deceased's feet, not Cob's, and one was loosely looped around his ankle. I wrote that Cob wore them and that ownership was disputed.
