# Memories
- [Cob's oversized boots](7bccb68a.md) — In Granary Yard, during Sir Bartholomew Puddle's questioning about the dead clerk's boots, I made a notebook entry after Cob answered.
- [Puddle offered Cob silver for the boots' location](b761b1e0.md) — In Granary Yard, I was present with my notebook while Puddle made Cob an offer and Cob answered.
- [Puddle paid Cob two crowns for the boots' location](4891a55f.md) — In Granary Yard, I watched and heard Puddle pay Cob after Cob's first answer about where he found the boots.
- [Moll said Prout jumped from the loft](8b3b24fc.md) — In Granary Yard, I heard Moll answer Puddle's question about who strung up Mister Prout and entered her claim in my notebook.
- [Puddle found the back doors locked](3d77475c.md) — In Granary Yard, I watched Puddle try the granary’s back doors after saying he would go up and reenact Prout’s leap; Moll was on the wall and Cob was across the yard.
- [Puddle reported marks and a head swelling on Prout](29e9c96a.md) — In Granary Yard, Puddle called down from the hoist while beside Prout; I was below with my notebook and recorded what he reported.
- [Puddle dropped Prout; I said I could sign murder](ddebc18c.md) — In Granary Yard, I was below the hoist as Puddle cut Prout's rope and failed to catch him; I examined the body and spoke to Puddle.
