Puddle bellowed from the hoist: “He has TWO neck marks! One slanty, one flat! Clearly he hanged himself twice” and also reported a lump at the back of Prout’s head and clean hands. I wrote down the two marks, swelling, and clean palms as reported by the investigator from the hoist; I did not examine them myself. I told him it was not a second hanging.
