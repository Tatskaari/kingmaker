From my place in the yard with my notebook, I heard Moll on the wall say: “I saw him jump. Right out of that door, at midnight. Wailing about his debts. Clear as day.” I wrote that the witness claimed the deceased jumped from the loft door. That is what Moll claimed; I did not witness the death.
