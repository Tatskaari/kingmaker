From my place on the wall, I saw Puddle put two crowns into Cob's hand. Puddle said it was his entire fortune, save for shillings and pennies, and asked where Cob found the boots. Cob pocketed the crowns and said, “In the yard, obviously,” then asked three crowns to look and said the boots stayed on.
