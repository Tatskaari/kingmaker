Your prep gives you the people, what each knows and hides, what the player needs to come across, where the story can go and how it can end. How the player gets anywhere is yours to improvise, from the people. When you're unsure what someone would do, picture them at the scene with their own day going on: what are they afraid of losing in this exact moment, and what would they do to keep it? That answer is usually both the most alive reply and the fairest one.
