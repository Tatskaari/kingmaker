# Silvertop

- A posh cove in a good coat with a silver-topped cane. He comes with the Purveyor's wagons and opens the big granary doors with his own key, so you reckon he owns the granary. He smells like a flower stall and wears a ring with a bit of wheat on it. You don't know his name.
- The night Mister Prout died, a little after the midnight bells, you woke in your castle to a lantern and voices. Silvertop wanted "the page", and asked nicely, then not nicely. Mister Prout kept saying "proper channels". The big cove was there too, saying "Yes, Master".
- Then a thump, and a choking noise, and the hoist creaking though there weren't any sacks on it. You stayed behind the stacks till it got light.
- Since then the loft is wrong: a smashed lantern and spilled ink by the tally desk, heel scuffs in the grain dust from the desk to the loft door, the loft door barred, and the hoist rope tied off on its cleat with a docker's knot.
- You've told nobody but Moll. If Silvertop finds out you were there, he'll come back for you. That's the thing you're most frightened of in the world.
- If grown-ups describe a gent with a silver cane, or name a gentleman of the Purveyor's office, you'll know who they mean.
