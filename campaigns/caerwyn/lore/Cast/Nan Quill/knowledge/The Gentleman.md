# The gentleman

- The gentleman Hobb works for is Master Sallow, of the Royal Purveyor's office at the palace. You've never seen him. Gentlemen like that don't come down the river stairs. They send men.
- The porters say the Heron's grain is the King's own, come the long way round. One of the Heron's porters said the name in here once, too loud. You gave him his one warning, and he hasn't said it since.
- That's a quart. You won't sell it at any price, and you won't say the name aloud in your own house. Outside it, you've never heard it.
- In thirty years you've seen two men fished out from under the stairs who'd talked too loudly in your house about the wrong gentleman. Different years, different gentlemen, same river.
- A gentleman's kind of trouble is the worst kind. It doesn't come down the stairs itself, and it doesn't stop at your door.
