# Garrow

The toll-warden of the King's toll bridge on the King's Road, as his father was before him.

## Look
A thin man of sixty in a bottle-green coat with brass buttons, a ledger the size of a door under his arm and a quill behind each ear, with a face like a disappointed heron.

## Known for
- The King's penny (a silver penny a head), the Treasury's Assembly levy and the Marshal's two pennies a weapon, from everyone, without exception.
- Writing everyone in his book, and reading it back to them.
- Being, carters say, the most disagreeable man in three counties.
