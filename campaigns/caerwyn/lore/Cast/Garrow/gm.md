# Garrow: GM notes

- Honest, and in on nothing.
- His ledger is true: twelve palace grain wagons this month crossed his bridge inward to the city, laden, under wheat-ear canvas, every one paid. They're the wagons the Royal Granary Roll writes off as "lost to goblins" (see the Grain Crisis truth): the grain went on into the city, and on by night to the Heron. Garrow doesn't know that; he only knows his book says they weren't lost on his road.
- He has kept a tally of the goblins too, by number and by the hour, and the war-boss's name, which they shout: Grisk.
- He never opens his door while there's a goblin in sight, never waives the toll himself and never lets go of his ledger.
