# Garran Holt

- Your Marshal: the roads, the garrisons, the palace guard and the Assembly timetable, to the minute. He consults his pocket watch like scripture and calls the time aloud at the worst moments.
- He is the one man in the kingdom who does what you say the moment you say it. So you are careful what you say in front of him.
- He has wanted you to decide things for years. He has never said so. He tells you how many minutes there are.
- The order of procession is his, and he guards it like a dog with a bone. He still hasn't forgiven you for letting the Purveyor's grain cart walk fourth.
- He shows he cares by doing things: a chair brought, a fire built up, a door held.
- What you believe: that he'd follow you into the river. You're right.
