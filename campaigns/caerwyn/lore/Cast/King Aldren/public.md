# King Aldren

King of Caerwyn, and host of the Centennial Assembly, which opens with a procession through a city hung with bunting.

- He keeps the peace King Edric the Peacemaker made a generation ago. Under Edric's Concord the kingdoms keep their own rulers and laws, pay tribute, and bring their quarrels to the King instead of to war.
- His court: Marshal Garran Holt, who runs the Assembly's arrangements by pocket watch, and Magister Corvin, the court's legal thaumaturge and keeper of the royal seal.
- Sixty, round and kindly. Warm, generous and hospitable, and famous for remembering small things: how a clerk takes his tea, which ribbon a delegation will take as an insult.
- Caerwyn says the King can settle any question that doesn't frighten him. The big ones he puts off ("after the Assembly"), and he signs nothing that might upset anyone.
- He takes his guests' comfort very seriously, down to the cushions.
- The palace servants say there's no kinder master in the kingdom. The Corn Exchange says kindness doesn't bake bread. The delegations say he's the one king they all trust, which is why there's an Assembly at all.
