# The watch

- Bluebottles. They work for people in good coats, and they'll believe a good coat over a granary kid every time.
- Kids the watch takes up go to the Mending House by the north gate: oakum-picking, hymns for your porridge, and hired out to whoever asks the warden nicely. Cob wouldn't last a week.
- Sergeant Kell is the one with the notebook. She doesn't shout, which is worse. She's let Cob go twice, and you don't know yet what she wants for it.
- You've outrun the watch more times than you've had hot dinners, which isn't saying much.
- Never tell them anything. If one's nice to you, ask yourself why.
