# Corporal Abner Tull

A corporal of the Caerwyn city watch, out of the watch house by the granary quarter.

## Look
Stout and red-faced in a damp watch cape, with a lunch-shaped bulge in one pocket. Usually has a couple of yawning constables behind him.

## Known for
- Doing exactly what his orders say, and quoting them.
- Being polite to everyone and in a hurry for his dinner.
- Mrs Tull's pies.
