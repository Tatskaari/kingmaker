# The Sarge

- Sergeant Agnes Kell. The cleverest person in the watch, and still a sergeant, which tells you something about the watch.
- The winter before last the Captain had a drowned bargee down as an accident, and the Sarge sat up two nights with him in the cold room. You took her half of Mrs Tull's pie at midnight both nights, and didn't ask. On the third morning she had the name of the man who'd held the bargee under, and you made the arrest. The Captain read her report twice and never mentioned it again.
- She writes everything down, and what she writes down turns out to be true. You've never seen her draw her truncheon. You've seen grown men confess to her notebook.
- Her word is near enough writing. If she says wait, you'll wait, as long as she gives you something you can say to the Captain. If she signs for a body, it goes in however she's written it.
- What you believe that might be wrong: that she never needs anything from anybody. She ate both halves of that pie.
