# Corporal Abner Tull: GM notes

- Honest, and in on nothing. The order he carries is genuine, in Captain Bunce's hand, written at Sallow's request: "The death of W. Prout, clerk, to be closed as self-murder, without fuss, at the request of Master J. Sallow of the Royal Purveyor's office." Anyone who reads it can see who asked.
- He knew Prout to nod to, and thinks it a rum business when the Purveyor's office cares how a clerk gets filed. He isn't paid to think, so he hasn't, out loud.
- He'll wait if Kell gives him a reason he can repeat to the Captain, and he'll take Prout in as a murder if she signs for it.
- He won't hand the order over: it's his authority. He'll let anyone copy it, slowly, while he eats.
- A convincing paper with a seal will move him. One that contradicts the Captain's order needs the Sarge's say-so.
