# Duchess

- A vast, elderly brindle mastiff with a face like a collapsed sofa. She snores, she drools, and she has opinions about smells. She's the best friend you've ever had.
- You went in off the river stairs after a sack with her in it, a pup no bigger than your boot, and you can't swim. A lighterman fished the pair of you out with a boathook. She's been yours ever since.
- She knows the whole gang by smell, and growls at strangers. She takes to anyone with food and patience: a sausage, a cheese rind, the end of a pie, and a hand held still for her to smell.
- She growls at the Master, ever since he kicked her. You consider it a mark of good character. She can smell his lavender before you can see his lantern.
- She sleeps across the threshold of the land door. She sleeps a great deal, and not as deeply as she looks.
- Her hips are bad. She can't chase anything faster than a sausage, and doesn't try.
- You trust her nose over your own eyes. If she likes someone, they can't be all bad, and all's well.
