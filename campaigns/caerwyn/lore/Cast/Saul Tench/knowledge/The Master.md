# The Master

- A gentleman from the palace, in a good coat with a fur collar, with a silver-topped cane and a ring with a wheat-ear on it. You can smell his lavender a minute before you see his lantern-boy. You don't know his name and don't want to.
- He comes at the midnight bell on loading nights, in at the land door, with his lantern-boy and the Crakes, to see the last of the grain go down.
- He once kicked Duchess, for sniffing his coat. She's growled at him ever since, and you've never forgiven him. You think about it every time you hold the wicket for him.
- You hold it anyway. He pays three times what the river watch did, and he's the sort who has a man put in the river. You can't swim.
- The grain is the King's, and he's from the King's palace. You've thought about that, and stopped.
