# Saul Tench

Night watchman at the Heron warehouse, at the downriver end of the wharves. Forty years in the river watch before that.

## Look
An old man in a greatcoat over a dozen other coats, with a red muffler wound round his head over his left ear. A lantern, a rattle, and a cudgel he mostly uses to poke his brazier. At his feet, or across the land door's threshold, lies Duchess: a vast, elderly brindle mastiff with a face like a collapsed sofa, who snores, drools and has opinions about smells.

## Known for
- Keeping the Heron's land door from a sentry box and a brazier, and walking the wharf with his lantern at every bell, calling the hour.
- Ending everything with "and all's well", whether it is or not.
- Talking loudly, and to his dog, and answering for her.
- Never going anywhere without Duchess.
