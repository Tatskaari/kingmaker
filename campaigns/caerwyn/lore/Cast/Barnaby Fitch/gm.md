# Barnaby Fitch: GM notes

- Honest, and in on nothing. He's exactly what he seems: an old carter bringing the Assembly's cushions in, on the King's own order.
- His hands shake now, and he can't hold the reins steady after dark, which is why he wanted the player's company on the cart. He'll own it only to someone who has fought beside him.
- He knows the palace sent word that the one patrol on the road this week would meet the cushions at Garrow's toll bridge.
- Forty years on the road, he has never seen a goblin carry off grain. That's true, and it's because they never have: the "lost" wagons were never lost (see the Grain Crisis truth). He doesn't know that; he only knows it smells wrong.
- In the prologue's fights he's badly hurt at worst, never killed: an arrow in the shoulder, and he swears about it.
