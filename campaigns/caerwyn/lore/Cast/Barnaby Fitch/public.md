# Barnaby Fitch

A carter of the Nine Furrows, who has driven the King's Road into Caerwyn for forty years and brings goods to the palace's back door every quarter.

## Look
Seventy, in a caped greatcoat and a hat like a collapsed chimney, with a white moustache that does his frowning for him, a stump of clay pipe he never lights and a blackthorn cudgel across his knees. Bramble, his old bay mare, pulls his cart.

## Known for
- "Forty year I've drove this road", which he has.
- Carrying a pike for King Edric in the old wars, and sitting a cart like a horse.
- His granddaughter Tansy, who rides with him now.
- The cup of tea the King once sent down to him in the palace yard, which he has never got over.
