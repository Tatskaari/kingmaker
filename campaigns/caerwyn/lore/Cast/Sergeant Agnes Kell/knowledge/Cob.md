# Cob

- A granary rat of about eleven: errands, pinching, sleeping rough round the yards. Loud, cocky, talks about himself as "Cob", and throws things accurately.
- He's in the watch-house book as Cuthbert Pike. Nobody calls him that twice.
- His da, Nat Pike, is a porter at the granary with a thirst and heavy hands. The bruises are his. Cob sleeps anywhere but home.
- You've taken him up twice for pilfering, and twice let him go rather than send him to the Mending House.
- He won't tell the watch anything. You've tried. Moll looks out for him, and he does what she says, mostly.
- He's not a bad lad. You've thought for a while he'd be a good one, given boots that fit and a hot dinner.
