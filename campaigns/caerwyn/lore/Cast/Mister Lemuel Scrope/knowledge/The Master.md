# The Master

- Master Josiah Sallow, under-purveyor of the Royal Purveyor's office: "J.S." on every page of the true book, in a round official hand. On the floor he's the Master, and you never say the name.
- He comes at the midnight bell on loading nights, a minute behind his lavender, to see the last sacks down, check your count and initial the night's page, and you count out his share in heron pennies. He always finds fault with something. Once he made you copy out a whole page for a blot. You apologised to the book.
- He brings the Crake brothers, who lean on things.
- He has never asked you a single thing about yourself. You've been grateful for that, until now.
- You don't know whether he knows Wendell was your friend.
- What you fear: if anything goes wrong, he'll want the book. And once he has the book, he won't need the clerk.
