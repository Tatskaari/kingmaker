# Mister Lemuel Scrope

Clerk to the Heron warehouse, keeping its Saltmere factor's books. He sits in the glass-sided counting-box over the warehouse floor.

## Look
Narrow and stooped, in rusty black, with sleeve-guards, a green eyeshade and a pen behind each ear. His left hand is always on a ledger.

## Known for
- Counting everything twice.
- Apologising to his ledger when he blots it.
- Being polite to everyone, and frightened of most of them.
- Keeping books on the wharves for one Saltmere factor or another these thirty-odd years.
