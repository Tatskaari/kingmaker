# Guardsman Tobias Wren

A guardsman of the palace guard, nineteen, on his first patrol outside the city walls. Corporal Maud Haskett's nephew.

## Look
Long and thin as a rake in a steel cap too big for him and the King's green, holding his spear like a broom, with a fluff of moustache he's very proud of and a face that goes white, then red.

## Known for
- "In the King's name!", at everything.
- Calling everyone "sir" or "madam".
- Thinking the world of the King, who knows his name and asks after his mother's chest.
