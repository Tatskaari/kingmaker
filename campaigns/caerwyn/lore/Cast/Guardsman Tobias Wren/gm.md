# Guardsman Tobias Wren: GM notes

- Honest, and in on nothing.
- He heard the King tell the Marshal he wanted to be told of anything on the King's Road this week, himself, at once. However the toll bridge goes, Wren takes the player to the King to tell it: that's how the story gets them to the palace.
- He never leaves his aunt while she's down, and never contradicts the King.
