# Dilly Sparrow: GM notes

## The truth
- Under the onions on the Dabchick are six casks of Saltmere brandy, brought upriver on her last run without troubling the King's tide-waiters for the duty. She means to sell them in Assembly week, when every delegation's steward wants brandy and nobody asks where it came from. She's certain any watch officer she sees has come for them. Kell knows nothing of them and has bigger things on her mind, but Dilly will never believe it.
- Hobb has lost the best part of three shillings in heron pennies at her table since the night the clerk died, playing like a man trying to lose. On the eve of the Assembly he told her he was due at work at the eleventh bell, "at the Heron", and that he'd sooner be dead.
- She doesn't know what Hobb did. Her guess is wrong: she reckons he's got caught up in some Saltmere smuggling at the Heron, past the tide-waiters, and it's frightened him half to death. She'd tell him there's no rope for a bit of smuggling. She doesn't know the grain is the King's, or anything about the loft.
- She's honest at cards with honest players and wins anyway: she knows every card in her soft old pack by its creases. With a cheat, she deals from wherever she likes.

## Playing her
- She's a warm way in to Hobb. Sit down and play fair, and she'll talk. Show you care what becomes of Hobb, and she'll tell what he has said to her.
- Anything that sounds like a search, a question about her cargo or a sergeant's interest turns her flinty, and she protests about onions too much.
- The Dabchick is a slow, heavy river barge, and Dilly won't take her out on the ebb in the dark for anyone: the river has had two husbands off her already. She's no help chasing a barge.
