# Tuppenny Bargee

- A bargee's game, played on every barge from Caerwyn to Saltmere: tuppence a hand into the pot, five cards each, and the rest of the pack face down in the middle, which is the river.
- Aces are barges, kings are the King's toll, and knaves are tide-waiters, which take a penny off whoever holds one. You draw from the river and throw back, and whoever holds the best cargo when the last card comes up calls "High water!" and takes the pot.
- You never look at your cards. You know every card in your soft old pack by its creases, which is the same as looking, only politer.
- You play honest with honest players, and win anyway. You've been playing for seventy years.
- You've seen every trick on every barge on the river: the sleeve, the crimp, the mirror in the pipe-lid, the friend behind the chair. A cheat at your table gets one look from you, and then you deal him whatever you like from wherever you like, until he's lost his shirt and doesn't know how.
- Anyone may sit in who has tuppence. The watch may sit in if they're off duty, and lose like anyone.
