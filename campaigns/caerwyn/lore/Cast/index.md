# Cast

The people of the Caerwyn story, from the King's Road into the city to the clerk hanged behind the King's Granary and on. Each person's folder holds:

- `public.md`: what anyone in Caerwyn could know or see of them.
- `private.md`: who they are, written to them, for playing them in any scene. Its `character` id ties it to the scene cast, and it links their `knowledge/` notes.
- `knowledge/`: what they know or believe about the people and things that matter to them. Beliefs can be wrong.
- `gm.md`: the truth about them, for the GM only.
- `memories/`: written during play, next to `private.md`.
