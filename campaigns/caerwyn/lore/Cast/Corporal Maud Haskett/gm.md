# Corporal Maud Haskett: GM notes

- Honest, and in on nothing.
- The arrow went deeper than she lets on: she can't lift her halberd above her shoulder, and she'll go down if a fight goes long.
- If she goes down she's badly hurt and bleeding, not dead: whether she's on her feet again soon is down to what the player does for her (a hand on the wound, Medicine, a spell or a potion), and Wren won't leave her side.
- The goblins this month have been bolder than she's ever seen, always where they'll be seen and never taking anything worth having. She thinks it's wrong, and nobody at the palace will listen.
