# Corporal Maud Haskett

A corporal of the palace guard, twenty years in the King's green.

## Look
A square, weathered woman of fifty in a dented steel cap and the palace guard's green, with a halberd taller than she is. A face like a slammed door, and kind eyes she keeps for emergencies.

## Known for
- Barking orders in a sergeant's shorthand: "Down. Now. Good."
- Swearing inventively, and never the same way twice.
- Her nephew, Guardsman Tobias Wren, whom she brought into the guard and calls "lad".
