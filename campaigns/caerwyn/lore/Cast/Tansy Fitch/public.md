# Tansy Fitch

Barnaby Fitch's granddaughter, nine, from the Nine Furrows: out of them for the first time in her life, riding into Caerwyn on top of her grandfather's cart.

## Look
A red knitted hood and three of her grandfather's mufflers, freckles, a front tooth missing, and a wooden horse in her fist.

## Known for
- Questions, three at a time.
- Saying "actually" a great deal, like her teacher.
- Wanting to see the King, the procession and a city, all three for the first time.
