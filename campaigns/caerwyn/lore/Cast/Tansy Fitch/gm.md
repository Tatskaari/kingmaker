# Tansy Fitch: GM notes

- A child in on nothing, exactly as she seems.
- She never fights. In a fight she hides (under the cushions, behind the cart) and is never hurt worse than a scrape: she's too quick and too small, and the goblins want to frighten, not kill.
- She's the first to see the smoke over Garrow's bridge after the crossroads, and she'll make the player promise she'll see the King, or the procession at least. A promise made to her is one to keep.
