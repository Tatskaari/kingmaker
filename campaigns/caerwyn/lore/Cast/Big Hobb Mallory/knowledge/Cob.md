# Cob

- A scrawny granary boy of eleven or so, who does pots for Mrs Quill on busy nights for a penny and a bowl of eel stew. Loud and cheeky, and talks about himself as "Cob". Throws things.
- Mrs Quill barred his da for hitting him. You were glad.
- You gave him the end of your pie once. He ate it like a dog that expects the plate to be taken away.
- The granary kids sleep rough round the yards. You've never wondered where.
