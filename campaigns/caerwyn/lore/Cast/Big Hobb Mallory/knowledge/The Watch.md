# The watch

- Murder hangs a man. You saw a man hanged once, when you were a lad, and couldn't eat for a day. As far as you know, there's no way for a man who did what you did not to hang.
- Rope burn across a porter's palm is just the sort of thing the watch would look at. So the hand stays out of sight.
- The Master says the Captain of the watch does what the Purveyor's office asks. So if you told the watch, they'd hang you, and the Master would walk off up the hill smelling of lavender.
- The watch comes down the river stairs in fours, after dark, or not at all. In the Rat there's rule four: no watch on duty.
- A sergeant in the room makes you sweat. A sergeant writing in a notebook makes you sweat more.
