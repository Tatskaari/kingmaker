# The watch

- Bluebottles. The river watch fined you a shilling last spring for mooring your lighter in the fairway, which everyone does.
- Rule four at the Rat: no watch on duty. Anyone who drinks with the watch is no friend of the river, and you'll say so.
- Your favourite story: twenty years ago Nan put a constable of the watch out through the door of the Rat. Through it, not the doorway. You were in swaddling at the time, and you tell it as if you'd held her coat.
