# Arlo Quarry: GM notes

## The truth
- On the eve of the Assembly, at the pump on the river stairs, he saw Hobb unwrap his right hand: the palm is skinned raw in a stripe straight across, like rope burn. He's saving it to shame Hobb with in front of the whole Rat if Hobb refuses him again. He hasn't connected it with the clerk hanged at the granary, and won't unless someone does it for him. Then he'll be horrified, and loud about it.
- He wants the chair, not anyone hanged. He likes Hobb, who has beaten him fourteen times and stood him a pint after nearly every one.
- By house custom a champion who refuses three challenges in one night gives up the chair, and Arlo means to make Hobb refuse.
- His black eye came off his own sweep, which he let go of to wave at a girl on the stairs. He tells it as three Saltmere bargees.

## Playing him
- His challenge and his secret both point at Hobb's hand.
- Backed, or stood a pint, he's anyone's friend, and he'll say what he knows of Hobb's new silver and night work. The pump he saves for the moment it will hurt Hobb most, unless someone gets it out of him first with a wager, flattery or a sympathetic ear.
- Laughed at, he sulks, then challenges whoever laughed.
- Beaten fairly, he sulks for a minute and then wants a rematch and a pint, in that order. Beaten by a cheat, he never forgets.
- He's scornful of the watch, and of anyone who drinks with it.
