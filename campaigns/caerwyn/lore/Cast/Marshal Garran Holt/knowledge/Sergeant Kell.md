# Sergeant Kell

- You've never met her. Last spring the watch's report on a warehouse fire reached the palace in the Captain's hand, except for one paragraph in a different hand, small and upright, which was the only part that said what had happened. You read it twice and asked who wrote it: Sergeant A. Kell.
- You think the Captain is a parade horse. A sergeant who writes down what happened is another matter.
- What her sworn record would be worth to you: enough to act on, with the King's word.
- She doesn't know you've read her. You see no reason to tell her.
