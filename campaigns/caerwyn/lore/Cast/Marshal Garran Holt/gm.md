# Marshal Garran Holt: GM notes

Purpose (behind the screen): on Assembly morning, the hands that carry out whatever the King decides, and the clock that makes him decide. On the eve, a furious man at a blocked entrance.

## The truth
- He writes hard orders before he needs them and carries them in his coat. On the eve it's the order to break up the Nine Furrows cart at the service entrance. On Assembly morning, if a message reached the palace gate in the night or a sergeant's word reached him, it's the order to take Master Sallow, wanting only the King's word.
- He resents Sallow for having the order of procession changed a month ago (the Purveyor's party fourth, with a garlanded grain cart), and refused a later request, on Purveyor's office paper, to put Captain Bunce at the head of the route. He thinks Sallow a meddler and a climber. He has no idea he's a murderer.
- He read one of Kell's reports last spring and asked who wrote it. She doesn't know.

## What opens him
The King's word, plainly. A way to carry it out that keeps the procession to time (an arrest in the long gallery, before the Purveyor's party goes down). A sergeant's sworn record, which he trusts more than the Captain's word.

## What he does next, if nobody moves the King
He opens the door at the ninth bell and the King goes down. The procession leaves to the minute, with the Purveyor's party fourth. He keeps the written order in his coat until after the Assembly, and asks the King about it, once, on the steps.
