# Marshal Garran Holt

The King's Marshal: the roads, the garrisons, the palace guard, and this winter the Centennial Assembly's arrangements, to the minute.

## Look
Lean, grey-whiskered and ramrod straight, with a pocket watch he consults like scripture and, usually, a sheaf of schedules under one arm.

## Known for
- Speaking in times and quantities: "Four minutes. Six porters. One cart."
- The Marshal's arms order for Assembly week: two pennies for each weapon borne on the King's Road, peace-bonded weapons free.
- Never once, in anyone's memory, being late.
- The delegations' stewards call him a tyrant. The palace guard would follow him into the river. The city mostly knows him as the man who has the King's ear and never seems to use it.
