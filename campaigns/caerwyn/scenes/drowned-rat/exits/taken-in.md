## Outcome

Sergeant Kell has arrested Big Hobb, on evidence she accepts, and is taking him up the river stairs to the watch-house cells. Whatever he told the player before he was taken, they have, and whatever he owned to is in Kell's notebook as he said it; he won't be at the Heron tonight.

## Closing

Close on the arrest done by the book outside Nan's door: Kell's proper words, Hobb's great shoulders going down, the Rat silent behind them, the river below. Kell will see him locked up and come on to wherever the investigator is going next.
