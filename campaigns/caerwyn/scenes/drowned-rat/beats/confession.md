Hobb is talking, or starting to. He begins where it hurts, with the rope, slowly and in pieces, wiping his face with his good hand, and he won't say more where the whole Rat can hear: the player has to find him somewhere quieter or give him a reason. The rest (his master, the Heron and tonight's loading, his token: see his secrets) comes out as the player asks for it, with no dice, one thing per reply and in his own words. He knows only his own part of the night, so he doesn't brief anyone on the Heron. Answer what they actually ask, and never put questions in their mouth or run the interview for them. If he thinks he's talking to his master's people (a trick that worked), he tells it as proof he has kept faith, and it's the same truth. Kell writes it all down. When it's out, he asks what's to become of him: he's terrified of the noose and more terrified of his master, and he has to be at the river door at the eleventh bell or his master will know something is wrong. The beat ends on the player's choice: Kell takes him in, he goes to work (as their man inside, or keeping his head down), or he runs. Don't decide it for them.
