## Look

A young lighterman built like a water butt, with rolled sleeves, a fresh black eye and forearms he keeps flexing. His elbow is on the nearest table at all times, in case.

## Voice

Loud, cheerful and spoiling for it. Talks in challenges and wagers ('Penny says I'd have you'). Calls everyone 'champ', sarcastically. Goes quiet and sulky when he's laughed at.

## Wants

The champion's chair, tonight, while Big Hobb won't wrestle: a champion who refuses three challenges in one night gives the chair up, by house custom, and Hobb is on two. And to win it fair, because he likes Hobb, who has beaten him fourteen times and stood him a pint after nearly every one, and a chair handed over by a man who won't wrestle isn't the same as beating him.

## Fears

Being laughed at. Being thought yellow. Nan, who has already given him his one warning tonight.

## Knows

- Hobb has been champion eleven years and has never refused a match until tonight. He keeps his right hand under the table.
- Hobb's been flush lately, with new silver, and working nights for a gent somewhere downriver.
- The house custom: elbows on the table, right hands, Nan referees, wagers paid at the bar.

## Secrets

- Shows: he keeps grinning at Hobb's hidden hand, like a man with a card up his sleeve. Opens: a wager, flattery or a sympathetic ear gets it out of him early; otherwise he saves it for the moment it will hurt Hobb most, the third refusal. First: 'I know what's under that rag, champ.'
- This evening, at the pump on the stairs, he saw Hobb unwrap his right hand: the palm is skinned raw in a stripe straight across, like rope burn. He hasn't thought about what a porter with rope burn has lost his grip on; told, he'll be horrified, and loud about it.

## Attitude

Friendly to anyone who'll wrestle him, back him or stand him a pint; scornful of the watch, and of anyone who drinks with it. Torn: all challenge and no menace, a big puppy rather than a wolf; it shows when he wins, or when Hobb looks frightened rather than proud. Beaten fairly, he sulks for a minute and then wants a rematch and a pint, in that order; beaten by a cheat, he never forgets. Ties here: Hobb, whose chair he wants and whom he doesn't want hurt.
