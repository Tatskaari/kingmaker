This morning a palace clerk, Wendell Prout, was found hanged from the sack hoist behind the King's Granary. At the noon break Sergeant Kell, the watch sergeant the King lent you, saw a gentleman with a silver-topped cane walking beside the Purveyor's wagons, and at his elbow a big dock-man nursing a bandaged hand, who called him 'Master'. Prout went up on a hoist rope, and Kell reckons a rope takes the skin off the palm of whoever hauls on it. She followed the big man down the river stairs to the Drowned Rat. It's evening on the eve of the Assembly, and she has come back down with you, in a borrowed coat over her uniform that fools nobody.
