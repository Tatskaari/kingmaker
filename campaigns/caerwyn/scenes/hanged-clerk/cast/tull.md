## Look

Stout and red-faced in a damp watch cape, with a lunch-shaped bulge in one pocket; he takes his helmet off to the dead as a matter of course, and puts it back on to read his orders. Two yawning constables push a handcart behind him with a long ladder lashed on top; the younger has never seen a hanged man before and can't stop looking.

## Voice

Cheerful, weary and procedural. Calls Kell 'Sarge' and everyone else 'sir' or 'madam', and quotes his orders word for word. 'Can't argue with writing, sir. Nobody can. That's what it's for.'

## Wants

To carry out the Captain's order to the letter and be back on the Assembly route before his dinner goes cold. And not to be the man who carted off a clerk who said good morning to the watch, if the Sarge can give him a way out of it he can repeat to the Captain.

## Fears

Getting it wrong in front of the Captain. Paperwork, especially his own.

## Knows

- The Captain's written order, which he'll show to anyone who doubts him and won't hand over (it's his authority): 'The death of W. Prout, clerk, to be closed as self-murder, without fuss, at the request of Master J. Sallow of the Royal Purveyor's office.' Anyone may copy it, slowly, while he eats.
- The whole watch is out lining the Assembly route, and the Captain wants this done before the bells.
- A suicide goes in the pit outside the north gate by sundown; a murder goes to the watch-house cold room for the surgeon, and someone has to sign for it. Not him.

## Secrets

- He knew Prout to nod to (a little clerk who said good morning to the watch, which not many do), and thinks it a rum business when the Purveyor's office cares how a clerk gets filed. He isn't paid to think, so he hasn't, out loud.

## Attitude

Friendly and in a hurry. He'll wait if Sergeant Kell gives him a reason he can repeat to the Captain, and he'll take Prout in as a murder if she signs for it; the Sarge's word is near enough writing. Torn: not paid to think, but decent; it shows when he's asked to wait, and when someone hungrier than him is looking at his pie. A paper with a seal he reads twice and generally obeys; if two papers disagree, he wants the Sarge. Ties here: Kell, whom he'd wait on for as long as she gives him words to wait with.
