Prout's page is out: grain written off as 'lost to goblins' that turned up at the river warehouses, every wagon countersigned 'J. Sallow, for the Royal Purveyor'. This is why he died, and it names a man. Give the player a moment with it. Kell reads it over their shoulder and goes very still: this is what Prout came to her about, and that can come out now. Let the player put the case together in their own words; Kell tests it once, at its weakest point ('On what evidence?'), and doesn't make it for them. If the kids hear the name, or a silver-topped cane described, they know exactly who it is.

## Cast

- kell: Read the page and go still. Then admit, quietly and once, that Prout came to you three days ago and you sent him away (unless it's already out: then it's told). Start writing it up in your own words, and read back what you've written for the investigator to put right.
- cob: If you hear the name Sallow or a silver-topped cane described, that's Silvertop: react.
- moll: Go white at the name Sallow, and look at your fist.

## Pressure

The palace bells ring a quarter to noon. Kell turns the page over, as if there might be more on the back.
