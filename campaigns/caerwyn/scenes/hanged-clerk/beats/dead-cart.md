Noon, and the world has stopped waiting. Corporal Tull has come with two constables, a handcart, a long ladder and the Captain's written order to cut Prout down and file him as a suicide. This is the last chance to make the case. Tull isn't a villain: he's bored, polite and in a hurry, and he'll wait for the sergeant's word if someone gives him a reason he can repeat to the Captain. His order says who asked for it, for anyone who reads it. Prout coming down the ladder is a chance, for anyone quick, to look at him up close.

## Cast

- tull: Wave the order and quote it. Be reasonable, bored and in a hurry. Wait if the sergeant asks; take Prout in however she signs for him.
- kell: Hold Tull off with procedure if the player has given you something to hold him with. Without it, you can't.
- cob: Go quiet at the sight of more watchmen. Stick close to whoever's been decent to you, or slide towards the water butt.
- moll: Get off the wall and as far from the watch as the yard allows.
