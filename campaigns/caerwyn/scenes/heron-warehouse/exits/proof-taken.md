## Outcome

The player has got out of the Heron alive with the true book: every 'lost' wagon by its Granary Roll number, with Sallow's initials on every page. Whatever becomes of the barge, the proof is in their hands.

## Closing

Close on the player in the dark with the book under their arm: the Heron's lanterns behind them, the river running, and whoever is with them. Let Kell read one page by whatever light there is and say something dry that is very nearly triumph.
