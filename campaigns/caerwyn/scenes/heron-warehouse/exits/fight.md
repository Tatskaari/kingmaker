## Outcome

A fight breaks out in the Heron between the player (and Kell, if she's with them) and whoever is on the floor to fight them: Master Sallow with his sword-cane and the Crakes, if he has come; if he hasn't come yet, Hep and her gang, with their knives and sack-needles, and Sallow and the Crakes aren't in it. It's in the grain dust and the swinging lantern light.

## Closing

Close on the moment the fight breaks out, with whoever is on the floor to fight it. If Master Sallow has come (he's here, a minute behind his lavender), his cane slides apart into a thin bright sword and the Crakes roll their shoulders. If he hasn't come yet, he isn't in this and doesn't come in now: Hep's gang put down their sacks and come with their knives and sack-needles, Hep bellowing them on. Either way: the dust in the lantern light, the lanterns swinging dangerously close to it, and anyone with any sense stepping smartly out of the way. End with 'Roll for initiative!'
