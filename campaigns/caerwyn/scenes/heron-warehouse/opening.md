An hour or so after the Rat, near midnight: open on the dark wharf with the Heron in view. Lantern light through the cracks with the grain dust swimming in it, the hoist swinging sacks down into the barge, Saul's brazier at the land door with Duchess snoring across the threshold, the river running high and black. Pick up from how the Rat ended: the player has come on Hobb's directions or on his heels, with his token or without. Kell is at their side, unless she took Hobb to the cells, in which case she's coming along the wharf to meet them. Don't show Master Sallow: he isn't here yet (and if Hobb ran to him from the Rat, neither is Hobb). Nobody gets a name until someone says it: describe the watchman, the dog, the forewoman and the clerk by look. End on something the player can act on: the watchman heaving himself up for his round, a knock and a token at the river door, the lamp in the counting-box window.
