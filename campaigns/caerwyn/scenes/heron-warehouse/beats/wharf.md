The player is outside the Heron on the dark wharf, and nobody inside knows they're here. This beat is for looking and choosing how to go in. Answer questions about the outside concretely and generously: the land door with Saul, his brazier and Duchess; the plank walk along the piles to the river door and its grille; the barge alongside and the hoist swinging sacks into her; the gang glimpsed through the river door, slitting sacks; the lamp in the counting-box window; the tide on the turn. Kell works out with the player where she's most use. Preparations the player makes should pay off in position and surprise, and a diversion (a noise down the wharf, a barge line slipped, something for Duchess to chase) can draw Saul or the gang away for a moment; a naked flame anywhere near the grain dust is a very bad idea, and everyone at the Heron knows it. Getting in is a real edge, but there are several doors into it, and some need no dice: Hobb's token at the river door, Hobb himself if he's on their side, or a sack on the shoulder and a place in Hep's short-handed gang.

## Cast

- saul: You want a quiet night: mind the land door, talk to Duchess, and get up at the bell to walk the wharf with your lantern.
- kell: Say where you'll be, once: left to choose, out here on the wharf with your whistle, where you can see who comes and goes; at the investigator's elbow if they want a witness inside.
- hep: You want hands and the count: drive the gang, and shout for help at the river door.
- hobb: If you're here, you're on the hoist. If you agreed at the Rat to let them in, you're listening for their knock.

## Pressure

Saul heaves himself up for his round and comes along the wharf with his lantern and Duchess, straight towards wherever the player is standing. Or a sack splits on the hoist and spills grain across the wharf at the player's feet, the wheat-ear stamp plain on the torn sacking.
