# The zoo

The zoo is where the game's features are laid out to see and try, one exhibit at a time. It isn't Caerwyn, and nothing done in it reaches the story's games: a visitor comes in as one of the ready-made characters (a fighter, a wizard or a rogue), sees an exhibit through, and goes again.

## The exhibits

- **The combat ring.** Raked sand inside a low stone wall, two stone pillars, an iron brazier and one gate. The visitor and Sergeant Kell fight whoever has been picked to stand on the far side: Master Sallow and the Crake brothers, the Crakes alone, or a pack of dogs off the wharves. Nobody dies in the zoo.
