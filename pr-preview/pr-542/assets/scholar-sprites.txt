Oswin, Rowan and Elinor — custom Kingmaker character sprites.

Generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool using the existing
Kenney Tiny Dungeon atlas as a visual reference. Selected by the author.
Prepared as 32x32 PNGs with nearest-neighbour sampling and a transparent
border-connected background. These are generated assets, not Kenney originals.

Design prompt: Three front-facing, squat pixel-art humans in the atlas palette:
Oswin, an elderly male professor in green/cream robes and a wizard cap;
Rowan, a young male agricultural wizard/inventor with brown hair, tan work
clothes, green mantle and tool belt; Elinor, a female university chancellor
with brown hair in a bun and purple/cream robes. Dark plum outlines,
minimal facial marks, hard square pixel clusters; no monsters or extra limbs.

The in-game files preserve the approved preview designs. A subsequent generated
background-extraction attempt returned an opaque checkerboard and was discarded.
