Centennial Assembly — custom Kingmaker character sprites.

Generated together with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool using the
original cast lineup and palace screenshot as style references. Selected by
the author. These are generated assets, not Kenney originals.

All 14 portraits use the same square frame, nearest-neighbour sampling to
32x32 and transparent border-connected backgrounds. The three initial scholar
portraits are superseded by this consistent full-cast set.

Design prompt: A single seven-column, two-row cast sheet with squat, broad,
front-facing figures, oversized heads, short limbs, dark plum outlines and a
shared muted palette. Aldren: crowned human king in gold/burgundy. Corvin:
grey-bearded human court mage in purple. Holt: human marshal in slate armour.
Elinor: female human chancellor with a bun and plum/cream outfit. Oswin:
elderly human professor with grey beard and green cap. Rowan: brown-haired
human inventor in tan/green. Gurt: crowned dwarf king with broad grey beard.
Klog: bearded dwarf official in a brown coat and cap. Bran: red-bearded dwarf
engineer in apron and metal cap. Peregrine: human prince in blue/gold.
Cressida: auburn-haired human woman in purple court dress. Abel: bearded human
sailor in navy/green. Palace guard: closed slate helmet and armour. Court
servant: grey hair, brown tunic and cream apron. No monsters or long legs.
