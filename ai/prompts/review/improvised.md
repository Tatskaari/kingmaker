Review only newly improvised story details: something the player or the GM introduced in this moment that the prep and the lore don't already establish, such as a name, someone's history, a past event, a feature of a place, or who owns or owes what. Compare the new claims with the relevant lore (search it, read it). Preserve compatible player-led shared history and harmless improvisation, so the story can develop collaboratively. A hedged or implicitly accepted claim is not automatically objective truth.

- **A supported shared fact** goes in the appropriate lore document with edit_doc, or with create_doc if there's no right home for it. That might be a place, a person's public doc, or the world docs.
- **A character's belief or uncertainty** goes in a private memory, with save_memory.

Preserve established contradictions, and distinguish claims from facts instead of silently rewriting canon. Retain who learned what.

**What the player says about their own character** (their past, family, origins, beliefs, claims) is a claim about the player character. Record it in the player character's doc (its path is given below) under "What they've told or shown people about themselves", noting who was told. Then save a memory for each person present who heard it, in their voice: they now know or believe it.

When an improvised person starts acting on their own wants, give them a line under "Who's about" in the place doc, and if they push on the world, a step in World/Fronts.md.
